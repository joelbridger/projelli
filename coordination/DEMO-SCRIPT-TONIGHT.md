# Advisor Prep Hero Demo Script for Tonight

## 30-second opening line

"Advisor Prep Hero is private AI for an advisory practice. It gives you one place to prep for a client, search their emails and documents, and send useful notes back to Wealthbox. Your files stay on your machine. When the AI uses a file, it shows where the answer came from."

## What to Click

1. **Start on the Clients list**

   Open Advisor Prep Hero on the Windows laptop. It should open to the Northcrest client list with about 80 sample households.
   Say: "This is a realistic sample practice. Each household has its own private space."

2. **Open the demo client**

   Click **Caldwell, Jennifer** in the client list.
   Point to the tabs across the client: **Client Map**, **Documents**, **Email**, and **Meetings**.
   Say: "This is the client workspace. Everything is organized around the household, not around random folders."

3. **Show email search, not AI email answers**

   Click the **Email** tab for Jennifer Caldwell.
   Search for **Roth conversion**. You should see 5 relevant emails.
   Say: "This is the part advisors live in. I can search the emails that belong to this client without digging through Outlook."
   If the Email tab is empty, do not panic. Click **Client settings**, then **Email accounts in this client**, then make sure **Microsoft 365** is checked. Tonight, Microsoft 365 is already connected as demo advisor Sarah Morgan.

4. **Show Ask as honest client prep**

   Click **Ask** in the left side navigation.
   Make sure the scope says **This client**.
   Ask: **What should I review before my next meeting with Jennifer Caldwell?**
   If the answer shows **General** and **No file match**, say: "This is the app being honest. It is telling me when it did not use a source. That matters more than pretending."
   Do not promise that Ask can cite emails tonight. The email memory fix is still being tested. Use email search for emails.

5. **Show the whole-practice switch**

   In Ask, click the **This client** dropdown.
   Point to **Whole practice**.
   Say: "This can also work across the whole practice, but I am keeping the demo focused on one client tonight."

6. **Show connected practice tools**

   Click **Settings**.
   Show the **Calendar** or **Scheduling** section.
   Say: "Microsoft 365 is connected for the demo advisor, so meeting and scheduling context can live here too."
   If you see Wealthbox in the connector area, say: "Wealthbox is connected too. The 80 households came from Wealthbox."

7. **Send to Wealthbox — CONFIRMED GREEN (tested end-to-end 2026-07-09 evening)**

   The full flow was proven on this laptop tonight, including the note arriving in the Wealthbox account (verified via the Wealthbox API). One tip: if the document still has unaccepted tracked changes, the Send quietly does nothing — click **Accept all** in the Review pane first, then Send.
   If confirmed, go back to **Caldwell, Jennifer**.
   Click **Documents**. Open a client note or Word document. Click **Send to Wealthbox**.
   Read the review card, then click **Approve**.
   Say: "This is the money moment. The advisor can prep inside Advisor Prep Hero, then send the finished note back to Wealthbox without copying and pasting."

## If Asked About Privacy

"Files stay local. When cloud AI is on, Advisor Prep Hero sends your question and only the needed context straight to your own AI account, not through our servers."

- Do not move the Microsoft 365 email mapping unless the Email tab is empty.
- If Ask says **General** or **No file match**, say that is the app being honest.
- Send to Wealthbox is confirmed green. If it seems to do nothing, accept tracked changes first (Review pane, Accept all).
- Do not call the 80 households real client data. Call them realistic sample households.
- Do not spend time in onboarding. Stay on Clients, Email, Ask, Settings, and Send to Wealthbox.
