# Share Purchase Agreement (Acme Corp) — p.7

ARTICLE 2. CLOSING.

2.1 Closing Date. The closing date of the transactions contemplated by this Share Purchase Agreement shall be March 14, 2026, or such other date as the parties may mutually agree in writing. The closing shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of signature pages.

2.2 Purchase Price. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares shall be Four Million Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($4,200,000), payable by wire transfer of immediately available funds at the closing.
