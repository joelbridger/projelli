# Jump (jump.ai) — Funding & Company Research

**Researcher stance:** Skeptical competitive intelligence. Public sources only.
**Access date for all sources below:** 2026-06-28 (today)
**Subject:** Jump — AI platform for financial advisors. Salt Lake City, UT.

> **Reading guide — two evidence tiers:**
> - **VERIFIED FACTS** = reported by independent third parties (trade press, an investor's own site, the T3/Kitces surveys) and/or internally consistent across multiple sources.
> - **JUMP'S OWN MARKETING CLAIMS** = numbers and superlatives that originate with Jump (its press releases, website, and blog). Note: even when a trade outlet repeats "27,000 advisors," the figure still *originates from Jump* and is **self-reported / not independently audited**. Funding amounts and lead investors, by contrast, are corroborated by the investors themselves and by multiple outlets, so they sit firmly in the VERIFIED tier.

## 4. ADOPTION CLAIMS ("35,000+" vs "27,000")

**This is the most important number to handle carefully — it is SELF-REPORTED by Jump in every instance.**

Timeline of the claimed advisor count (all originate from Jump):
- **Seed (~early 2024):** "hundreds of RIA and Broker-Dealer advisors" — MARKETING.
- **Series A (Feb 3, 2025):** no single headline count; claimed "**average monthly growth rate of over 35%**" since leaving closed beta (Jan 2024) — MARKETING.
- **Series B (Feb 19, 2026):** "**27,000+ advisors** on the platform," "**2,000+ new advisors/month**," and "nearly **1 in 10** U.S. financial advisors now uses Jump." Jump also cited ~**$12 trillion** in client assets touched — ALL MARKETING (self-reported).
- **March 2026 product PR:** still "**27,000+ advisors**."
- **CURRENT (jump.ai homepage, June 2026):** "**Trusted by 35,000+ advisors**, rated #1 across T3, Kitces, and The Oasis Group reports." — MARKETING (self-reported).
- Some June 2026 coverage paraphrases "**more than 30,000 advisors**."

**So the current headline claim is "35,000+" (homepage), up from "27,000+" at the Feb 2026 Series B.** The memo's "35,000+ OR 27,000" reflects exactly this drift; the *latest* marketed figure is **35,000+**.

**THIRD-PARTY corroboration of the count:**
- There is **no independent audit** of the raw 35,000/27,000 figure — it is Jump's own telemetry.
- **Indirect** third-party support exists: the **2025 T3/Inside Information Software Survey** and **2025 Kitces Report** (both independent industry surveys of advisors) ranked Jump #1 in advisor-AI adoption/satisfaction, and **Ezra Group's 2026 report** is cited as saying Jump has "**nearly 1 in 10** U.S. financial advisors — the largest market share of any advisor-specific AI platform." These corroborate *category leadership* but **not the exact headcount**.
- Source (homepage): https://jump.ai/ (accessed 2026-06-28); Series B count via https://www.insightpartners.com/ideas/jump-raises-80-million-series-b-led-by-insight-partners.../ (accessed 2026-06-28)

## 8. PRICING

- **CURRENT (jump.ai/pricing, accessed 2026-06-28) — Jump's own page:**
  - **Meet (core):** **$100 per advisor/mo** — AI notetaker, unlimited meetings, prep, follow-ups, CRM/financial-planning sync, scheduling.
  - **Onboard (add-on):** **$50 per advisor/mo** — AI account opening, form completion, document intake.
  - **Grow (add-on):** **$50 per advisor/mo** — meeting analytics, coaching, revenue-opportunity sourcing.
  - **Enterprise:** custom pricing (SSO, SCIM/SAML, compliance dashboard, custom disclosures).
  - **Annual discount:** "Save up to 20% with annual payments."
- **THIRD-PARTY (InvestmentNews, "Is $50 the new $120?", accessed 2026-06-28):** Jump's tiers start at ~**$75/mo** (basic), with a **~$120/mo** tier that adds meeting "scorecards." The article frames the broader market as undergoing **price compression** — integrated platform players **Altruist and Wealthbox at ~$50/mo** are undercutting the original ~$120/mo standalone price.
  - https://www.investmentnews.com/advisor-tech/is-50-the-new-120-price-compression-comes-to-ai-notetakers/264773 (accessed 2026-06-28)
- **Verdict on the memo's "~$75–120/advisor/mo":** **Roughly accurate** historically (standalone tier ran $75 basic → $120 premium). But the **current jump.ai page shows a $100 core ("Meet") + two $50 add-ons** structure, so a fully-loaded Jump seat can reach **~$200/advisor/mo** (Meet+Onboard+Grow), while the entry point is ~$75–100. Note the downward market pressure.
