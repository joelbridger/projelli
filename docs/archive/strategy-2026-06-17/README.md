# Archived — the 2026-06-17 "product is mature, stop building" strategy cluster

**Superseded. Kept for history.** These four documents are the strategic-evaluation
output from **2026-06-17**. Their central conclusion — *"the product is mature; the
binding constraint is distribution and trust, not engineering; stop building"* — was
**overturned**, twice:

1. **2026-06-20:** hands-on testing on real Windows hardware found many unfinished and
   broken areas, so "the product is finished" was wrong. Finishing and hardening the
   product is real, necessary work — alongside, not after, distribution.
2. **2026-06-23 + 2026-06-29:** the product re-aimed to **financial advisors**, and the
   board decided to compete head-on as a simple AI-first app — the opposite of this
   cluster's "retreat to a narrow litigation niche" recommendation.
