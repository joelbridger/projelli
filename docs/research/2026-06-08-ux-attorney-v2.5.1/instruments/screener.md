# Recruiting Screener: Advisor Prep Hero Attorney Study (Pass B)

**Purpose:** Recruit 5-7 qualified attorneys for the live v2.5.1 study. Use as-is when the build ships. Target a mix; do not fill all slots with one practice area or firm size.
**Incentive:** $200 honorarium per 60-minute session (two sessions = $400), or pro-rated. Attorneys value their time in real dollars; under-paying biases the sample toward the under-employed.
**Method:** Remote, screen-shared, recorded with consent. ~60 min interview, ~60 min usability (can be same day with a break, or two sessions in one week).

---

## Intro (read to candidate)

"Thank you for your interest. We're doing a paid research study with attorneys about how you work with documents, email, and AI tools. This is research, not a sales call, and nothing you say will be shared with your clients or firm. I have a few quick questions to see if it's a fit. About five minutes."

---

## Qualifying questions

**Q1. Are you a practicing, licensed attorney?**
- Yes → continue
- No → thank and end

**Q2. How would you describe your firm?**
- Solo practitioner → **qualify (priority)**
- Small firm, 2-10 attorneys → **qualify (priority)**
- Mid-size, 11-50 → qualify (cap at 1-2 of sample)
- Large firm, 50+ → screen out (not ICP)
- In-house / government → screen out for this study (different buying + IT context)

**Q3. What's your primary practice area?** *(Recruit a mix; aim for at least 3 litigation-heavy and 2 transactional/advisory.)*
- Litigation (civil, employment, PI, commercial) → priority
- Transactional / business / real estate → include
- IP / patent → include if available (distinct needs)
- Estate / family / general practice → include
- Criminal defense → include 1 if available

**Q4. How involved are you in choosing the software tools you use?**
- I decide / I'm the decision-maker → **qualify**
- I have significant input → qualify
- Someone else decides → screen out (we need buyers)

**Q5. Roughly how many emails do you handle on a normal workday, and where does your email live?**
- *(Looking for heavy email users on Microsoft 365 / Outlook, Gmail/Google Workspace, or IMAP. Note their provider; we need at least 2-3 on Microsoft 365 since that's the lead connector.)*
- Screen out anyone who says email is not central to their work.

**Q6. Have you ever tried an AI tool (ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, etc.) for any work task?**
- Yes, use it regularly → qualify (note: AI-comfortable)
- Tried it, stopped or use rarely → **qualify (priority: richest tension)**
- Never tried → qualify (cap at 1-2; we want some, not all)
- *(Recruit a spread across all three. Do not over-index on power users.)*

**Q7. Have you ever worried about client confidentiality or privilege when using, or considering, an AI or cloud tool?**
- *(Open. We want people who can speak to the confidentiality tension, but don't lead. Note their framing.)*

**Q8. What computer do you primarily work on?**
- Windows → qualify (lead platform)
- Mac → qualify
- Chromebook / tablet only → screen out (desktop app required)

**Q9. Articulation check (do not score out loud):** "Tell me about the last time a piece of software frustrated you at work." *(We need participants who can narrate their experience. Screen out one-word answerers; keep vivid storytellers.)*

---

## Logistics

**Q10. Availability** in the next two weeks for one or two 60-minute video sessions.
**Q11. Tech check:** Can join a screen-share call on their work computer, and willing to install a signed desktop app (we provide it and a clean test license; they will NOT use real client data during the session).
**Q12. Confirm** honorarium, recording consent, and that they are not employed by or invested in a competing product.

---

## Quota grid (target n = 6, floor 5)

| Dimension | Target spread |
|---|---|
| Firm size | 3-4 solo, 2-3 small firm (2-10) |
| Practice area | ≥3 litigation, ≥2 transactional/advisory, 1 IP if possible |
| Email provider | ≥3 Microsoft 365, 1-2 Gmail/Workspace, 1 IMAP/other |
| AI exposure | ~2 regular users, ~2 lapsed/skeptical, ~1-2 never |
| Platform | Mix of Windows and Mac, Windows-weighted |

---

## Screen-out summary
Large firms (50+), in-house/government, non-decision-makers, tablet-only users, anyone with no email pain, anyone affiliated with a competitor, and non-narrators.
