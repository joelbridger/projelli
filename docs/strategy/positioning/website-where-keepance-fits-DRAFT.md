## Where Keepance fits

*A private AI that actually knows your clients.*

You already pay for a dozen tools, and I'm not asking you to drop any of them. Your CRM holds the notes. Your planning software holds the plan. Holistiplan reads the return. Schwab holds the accounts. The real story of a client is spread across all of it, plus years of email and a folder full of PDFs. No single tool has the whole picture, and the one AI that could help you find it is the one place you can't safely put a client's private information.

Keepance sits quietly on top of the tools you already use. It reads across your files, your email, and your client folders, and it answers any question about a household in seconds. Every answer comes with a citation you can click, so you can check it before you trust it. When you're ready, it drafts the review letter or the follow-up note from the real file instead of a blank page.

And your client data stays on your own computer. Keepance never keeps it on a server. You can run the AI on your own machine, or connect your own AI account, but either way nothing about your clients passes through me.

It doesn't replace your CRM, your planner, your custodian, or your note-taker. It's the one place everything about a client comes together and answers back. You don't switch away from anything. You finally get answers across all of it.
