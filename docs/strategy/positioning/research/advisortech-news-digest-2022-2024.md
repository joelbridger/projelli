# AdvisorTech News & AI-Feature Digest — 2022-2024 (from Kitces monthly roundups)

*Source: Michael Kitces' "The Latest In Financial #AdvisorTech" monthly columns, January 2022 – December 2024 (36 issues). Compiled as positioning research for Advisor Prep Hero (private, local-first AI that reads across an advisor's files/email/tools and answers with citations).*

---

## The arrival of AI in advisor tech (narrative)

Through most of **2022, "AI" in advisor tech meant document-scanning, not generative AI.** The template was **Holistiplan** (founded 2019 by Roger Pine and Kevin Lozer): an advisor uploads a PDF tax return, and the tool extracts the figures and surfaces tax-planning opportunities in minutes instead of the 1-2 hours of manual review. By 2021 Holistiplan was the breakout AdvisorTech story; by 2023 it had 20,000+ advisor users and ~42-62% category adoption. This "scan a primary-source document and summarize it" pattern spawned a wave of imitators that the industry literally nicknamed "Holistiplan for X": **FP Alpha** launched its **Estate Snapshot** in May/June 2022 (AI reads uploaded wills, trusts, powers of attorney; produces a summary of executors/trustees/beneficiaries, asset-transfer flows, and planning opportunities), then a **P&C Insurance Snapshot** at T3 in March 2023 (reads home/auto/umbrella policies, flags coverage gaps); **VRGL** (Series A $15M, Sept 2022) scanned brokerage/account statements to extract holdings, cost basis, and fees; and **Fidelity Labs' Saifr** (March 2022) used AI to triage marketing copy for compliance. So the *first* "AI" advisors actually adopted was narrow, extractive document AI.

**Generative AI arrived with ChatGPT (launched November 2022)** and showed up in Kitces' columns in early 2023. Kitces' framing crystallized fast: AI would **replace advisor support staff (back/middle office), not advisors**, and the tools that worked were "**word calculators**" — they draft editable text on a blank page — not "advice engines." The first wave of generative features (visible March-July 2023) bolted ChatGPT onto existing tools: **Orion/Redtail Speak** suggested replies to client texts; **Pulse360's "AI Writer"** turned shorthand meeting notes into client-ready copy; **FMG** and **Hearsay** generated compliant social-media posts; **Catchlight** wrote personalized prospect outreach. The first **standalone AI writing tool for advisors, Wealth Management GPT** (Marc Butler, ex-Skience), launched August 2023.

**The AI notetaker category exploded across 2023-2024.** **Zocks** appeared at T3 in early 2024; by mid-2024 Kitces was tracking a crowd of advisor-specific meeting-notes tools — **Jump** (raised $4.6M, won Best-in-Show at WealthStack 2024, ~$100+/user/mo), **Zocks**, **Finmate.ai**, **Vega**, **Filenote.ai**, **FieldKo**, **Focal**, **Thyme**, **Mili**, and **Zeplyn** (seed $3M, Dec 2024). By December 2024 Kitces counted **9 advisor-specific AI notetakers** plus general tools (Fathom, Otter, Fireflies, Zoom). These tools do meeting prep from CRM data, transcribe the meeting, write the follow-up email, log notes to CRM, and assign tasks. Enterprises built their own: **Morgan Stanley** (OpenAI partnership announced March 2023) shipped an **AI @ Morgan Stanley Assistant** (Sept 2023, research over ~100,000 internal documents for 16,000 advisors) and then **"Debrief"** (Sept 2024, AI meeting notes drafting follow-ups into Salesforce).

**Document-extraction AI also went generative-grade by 2024:** **Powder** (seed $5M, Aug 2024, $500/seat/mo) used AI to parse statements and estate docs and even *fill in missing data* (e.g., missing ticker symbols); **Wealth.com's "Ester"** (Aug 2023) read estate documents and showed a confidence score; **Nitrogen's AI Statement Capture** (beta, Nov 2024) and **YCharts** added AI statement extraction; and **Holistiplan announced an Estate Module** (Nov 2024, scanning estate docs to visualize asset distribution and beneficiaries) — Holistiplan finally fulfilling its long-stated "holistic" ambition.

**On regulation,** the SEC moved early and aggressively. It proposed the broad **Predictive Data Analytics (PDA) "AI" rule in July 2023** (would force advisers/BDs to "eliminate or neutralize" conflicts from any "covered technology" predicting/guiding investor behavior — so broad it arguably swept in rebalancers, risk-tolerance tools, and planning software). The SEC and **Massachusetts' Galvin** (Sept 2023, letters to JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, Tradier, US Tiger, Savvy Advisors, Hearsay) requested details on firms' AI use. Then in **March 2024 the SEC brought its first "AI-washing" enforcement** — fining two firms a combined **$400,000** for false/misleading claims about using AI (one had called itself the "first regulated AI financial advisor"). Kitces' read throughout: the regulatory pressure pushes AI *away* from advice-giving and *toward* back-office support — exactly where it was already winning.

## Chronological highlights (newest first)

### December 2024
- **Robinhood acquires TradePMR for $300M.** Robinhood: 25.1M retail accounts, $152.2B AUM (Q3 2024, ~$6,000/account); 75%+ Millennial/Gen Z. (Schwab: 36M accounts/$9.9T; Fidelity: 38.7M/$15T.) TradePMR is an independent RIA custodian, currently clears through Wells Fargo. Robinhood paid $65M to SEC (2020) and $70M to FINRA (2021) in prior settlements. **Kitces:** the deal isn't about competing with Schwab/Fidelity for custody — it's about keeping wealthy Robinhood customers on-platform by referring them to on-platform RIAs; mega-RIAs will rush into the referral system. Schwab considering raising its referral revenue-share % after being flooded with demand.
- **Advyzon to launch "Auria"** — family-office platform for UHNW: portfolio management, performance reporting, CRM; tracks traditional + alts (PE, real estate), complex ownership (layered trusts/LLCs). Competes with Addepar and Black Diamond. **Kitces:** CAIS/iCapital are democratizing alts for non-UHNW, narrowing the UHNW-vs-mainstream gap.
- **SmartAsset** launched two prospecting tools — **"Activity Report"** (tracks advisor follow-up with leads) and **"Pipeline Report"** (measures paid-lead-to-client conversion); follows 2024's Advisor Marketing Platform (AMP) and a "Business Development Startup Kit" for new RIAs.
- **[AI] Zeplyn** raised **$3M seed** — advisor AI meeting notes, differentiating on transcription accuracy. Kitces lists 9 advisor-specific AI notetakers: Jump, Zocks, Finmate AI, Focal, Filenote AI, FieldKo, Zeplyn, Thyme, Mili (plus Fireflies/Fathom/Zoom). Standard price ~$100+/mo. **Jump and Zocks have a substantial adoption lead.**
- **[AI] Advisor CRM** launched — new CRM founded by two advisors from RIA Fusion Capital Management; adds workflow/task management, digital account opening, trading management, and an **AI writing aid for follow-up emails**; all-in-one like Advyzon. (Redtail $39/user/mo; Wealthbox $59/user/mo; Salesforce much higher.)
- **Ezra Group** launched a self-service **Product Integration Portal** where vendors update their own data feeding the 10-point **Integration Score** (covers number/depth of integrations, API availability, SOC 2/data privacy); 500+ solutions rated; scores appear in the Kitces AdvisorTech Directory.
- **Kitces:** AI notetaking is too crowded for all to survive standalone — likely outcome is acquisition by CRM providers; CRM category is ripe for disruption as advisors build their own.

### November 2024
- **[AI] Holistiplan Estate Module** announced (2025 launch) — scans/extracts **estate planning documents**; outputs a visualization of how assets distribute at death plus named beneficiaries on retirement/TOD accounts; aimed at mass-affluent (simple estates). (Original tax-return scanner launched 2019.) Only 11% of advisors use a standalone estate tool. Competes with FP Alpha, Powder. **Kitces:** less pent-up demand than the tax tool because estate docs update every 10-15 years vs. annually.
- **[AI] Nitrogen "AI Statement Capture"** (beta) — scans client investment statements into Nitrogen's analytics + proposal flow (bundled into Risk Center; analysis centers on its "Risk Number"). Directly competes with VRGL ("5-Pillar"). **YCharts** also rolled out AI statement extraction. **Kitces:** one of the first established platforms building in-house to compete with a standalone tool; predicts CRMs (Wealthbox/Redtail) add AI meeting notes next.
- **CurrentClient** won **Best-in-Show at XYPN Live AdviceTech** — advisor texting/phone platform; captures messages to CRM/archiving, dedicated business line, one-to-many + auto-scheduled texts (e.g., birthdays), **AI call transcription/summaries**, **AI writing assistant**. Context: SEC has levied ~**$2.7B** cumulative in "off-channel" comms fines since 2021. Competes with MyRepChat, MessageWatcher, Google Voice, RingCentral, Celltrust.
- **Quivr** won **"Advicer's Choice"** at XYPN — CRM on a Salesforce chassis, pre-loaded with 35 advisor workflows; **$250/month** (vs. Redtail $39, Wealthbox $59). Founded by advisor Steve Drost.
- **Farther** raised **$72M Series C** (CapitalG + Viewpoint Ventures); cumulative $118M; **$5B AUM** (up from $1B Sept 2023). Digital RIA building its own stack; growth driven by recruiting advisors with cash/equity. **Kitces:** growth is traditional recruiting, not tech-enabled margin.
- **Freewill launched "Estately"** — advisor-facing estate documents (self-directed for simple plans + live-attorney network for complex). Launch partner Mercer Advisors. Competes with Trust & Will, Wealth.com, Vanilla.
- **Kitces:** industry profit margins drifted from 40-30-30 to ~35-40-25 despite decades of tech, because firms reinvest efficiency into more services rather than banking margin.

### October 2024
- **Wealth.com** raised **$30M Series A** led by **Google Ventures (GV)** — Google's early AdvisorTech entry (prior $16M seed). Digital estate documents + new Family Office Suite; ~**$5,000/advisor/year** for unlimited estate docs. Competes with Vanilla, EncorEstate, Trust & Will.
- **VRGL** raised ~**$6M** (prior: $16M Series A 2022) — scans prospect investment statements (positions/holdings/market value/cost basis), runs "5 Pillars" (performance, risk, diversification, fees, tax), generates proposals; exports to YCharts (API) or Bloomberg/Morningstar (spreadsheet).
- **[AI] Cashmere** raised **$3.6M seed** (after a $4.6M seed in May 2024) — AI prospecting; uses public data (IPO filings, capital raises, real-estate listings) to find money-in-motion events, infers an advisor's preferred client profile from existing data, auto-ranks prospects. Competes with Catchlight, WealthFeed, AIdentified, Wealthawk, Finny.
- **Fidelity blocks Pontera-style credential-sharing** — Fidelity (largest 401(k) provider, >$3.4T DC assets, 30M participants) said it will "prevent platforms reliant on credential sharing" from acting in its accounts. **Kitces:** likely more competitive than security-driven; may push advisors to roll Fidelity 401(k) assets away faster.
- **Schwab shuts its B2B robo (Institutional Intelligent Portfolios)** — closed to new accounts Nov 15, 2024; full shutdown 2025; offers advisors iRebal instead (proposes, doesn't execute). Remaining B2B robos: Betterment for Advisors, AdvisorEngine. **Kitces:** B2B robos failed because the problem was client acquisition, not portfolio efficiency.
- **Income Lab** launched an **Annuity Planning** tool — models specific annuity features (benefit basis, annuitization factors, participation rates, caps) inside the retirement picture.
- **Kitces:** estate-document market is structurally small — even 100% CFP adoption is maybe a $100-200M market.

### September 2024
- **Fidelity + Advyzon bundle** — Fidelity packages its Wealthscape brokerage + eMoney planning with **Advyzon** portfolio management/reporting (20% Advyzon discount); separate FMAX SMA bundle. Rumored $100M+ AUM custody minimum. (Wealthscape launched 2016, reportedly ~$2.5B tech budget, meant to kill third-party PM software.) Advyzon was the top-rated reporting tool in 2023 Kitces study. **Kitces:** signals Wealthscape failed its mandate.
- **GeoWealth** received **$18M from BlackRock** (a month after a partnership offering direct indexing, fixed-income SMAs, and alts through GeoWealth); will build UMAs. Fast-growing tech TAMP. **Kitces:** "$18M is pocket change for a $10T firm"; analogizes to studios owning theaters.
- **Vanilla** raised ~$20M (total now >$66M, prior $46.4M) — estate platform; Document Builder (trusts/wills) launched late 2023. Founder Steve Lockshin. Competes with Wealth.com, EncorEstate, Trust & Will. **Kitces:** limited by 5-10 year estate-doc update cycle.
- **Hearsay Systems sold to Yext for $125M** (+ up to $95M earn-out). Hearsay had raised $51M (Sequoia-led, early 2010s), peaked at $171M valuation (2013); at sale: $60M revenue, 260,000 advisor/agent users. **Kitces:** overfunding risk even for successful AdvisorTech.
- **[AI] Wealthtender + Hadrius** — a **Testimonial Compliance Scanner**: AI scans testimonials collected via Wealthtender against the SEC Marketing Rule and flags violations.
- **[AI] Morgan Stanley "Debrief"** — internal AI meeting notes (built on OpenAI, on MS's own data across 16M clients): writes meeting summary, drafts the follow-up email, saves notes to Salesforce. Second product after the Sept 2023 research assistant; OpenAI partnership announced March 2023. Keeps all data in-house.

### August 2024
- **Orion unbundles its all-in-one** — already split out Risk Intelligence (ex-Hidden Levers) and compliance (ex-BasisCode); now adds standalone trading/rebalancing (ex-Eclipse). Admits only ~20% of advisors use a fully integrated stack.
- **Nitrogen (ex-Riskalyze) unbundles** into 3 products — Risk Center (risk tolerance/proposals), Research Center (research/models), Planning Center (accumulation/distribution). Re-launched the original Riskalyze risk tool standalone at its decade-ago **$99/month**.
- **Envestnet goes private** — **$4.5B take-private by Bain Capital** at **$63.15/share** (~30% below the 2020 peak of $89.04; only ~10% above 2015's ~$57). Fidelity + BlackRock join the ownership group. IPO'd 2010; history includes Tamarac, MoneyGuide, Yodlee ($590M). **Kitces:** expects divestiture of Yodlee/MoneyGuide/Tamarac; Envestnet never made the whole worth more than the parts (the "Great Unbundling").
- **Altruist** — (1) high-yield cash account at **5.1% APY** (launched March); (2) **TaxIQ** tax-sensitive feature with optional tax-loss harvesting at **10bps/yr** on taxable accounts (free if using Altruist's model marketplace). Shift from indirect (cash sweep) to direct (platform fee) revenue.
- **[AI] Powder** raised **$5M seed** — AI document parsing for **investment statements AND estate docs**; beyond OCR, it *fills in missing info* (e.g., missing ticker symbols) and augments data. **$500/seat/month.** Competes with Holistiplan (tax), FP Alpha (tax/insurance/estate), VRGL (statements).
- **Future Capital launched "Construct"** — competes with Pontera for held-away 401(k) management (Pontera, ex-FeeX, had been the only major player for 6 years; charges 30bps on new accounts).
- **RISR** raised **$1.5M pre-seed** — business-owner engagement: basic business valuation, QuickBooks integration, **optical scanning of business tax returns**. Competes with BizEquity, Capitaliz.

### July 2024
- **[AI] Jump** raised **$4.6M** — advisor AI meeting support (CRM-fed prep summaries, note logging, follow-up emails, task assignment); integrates Wealthbox/Redtail/Salesforce; **$100+/user/mo**; won Best-in-Show at WealthStack. Competitors: Zocks, Finmate.ai, Vega, Filenote, FieldKo.
- **AIdentified** raised **$12.5M Series B from FactSet** — digital prospecting that maps 2nd/3rd-order network connections and tracks money-in-motion within that web.
- **[AI] Brightwave** raised **$6M seed** (6-person team) — AI **investment research assistant**: scours/synthesizes market data into investment insights and client-facing market commentary/marketing.
- **Pontera regulatory pressure widens** — Missouri sent warning letters to ~45 state-registered RIAs about credential-sharing tools (broader than Washington's late-2023 Pontera-specific action).
- **RIA Growth Catalyst** launched — uses public Form ADV data to identify RIA acquisition targets (organic growth, productivity, client types, holdings); **$10,000+** access.
- **FTSE Russell direct-indexing survey** (631 advisors): only **21%** use direct indexing; 48% plan to in 5 years; 34% cite lack of client demand.

### June 2024
- **Altruist** raised **$169M** (announced May) — total raised **$450M**, valuation **$1.5B**; now clear **#3 RIA custodian** behind Schwab & Fidelity (broke from Apex early 2023, built own custody/clearing). Schwab has ~15,000 RIAs. Limits: no retail base, no referral program, no consumer brand.
- **Archive Intel** raised **$1M seed** — compliance archiving beyond email/social to **iMessage** (now) and **WhatsApp** (coming). Context: SEC's **$81M** Feb 2024 penalty across 16 firms + LPL's **$50M** March 2024 settlement for off-channel comms.
- **RightCapital "RightRisk"** — built-in risk tolerance using the 13-question **Grable & Lytton scale** (1999); free in premium/platinum tiers. Competes with PreciseFP, DataPoints, FinaMetrica. **Kitces:** RightCapital keeps absorbing standalone categories (Blueprint, visualization, now risk) — ominous for standalone risk tools.
- **[AI] Couplr + American College of Financial Services** — white-labeled AI advisor-matching engine powering a "find-an-advisor" platform for ChFC/RICP/WMCP designees. (CFP Board spends ~$16M/yr on its own consumer platform; most certificants get only 1-2 clients from it.)
- **AI meeting-notes category** named: Finmate.ai, Jump, Vega, Filenote.ai, Zocks, FieldKo (+ Fathom/Otter/Fireflies/Zoom). Kitces productivity research: advisors spend ~1 hr prep + ~1 hr follow-up per 1 hr of meeting.

### May 2024
- **NewRetirement** raised **$20M Series A** — 70,000 active users; 23,000 paying $10/mo; also a $1,500 human-planning engagement; ~$100B potential assets. Distribution via 401(k) recordkeepers + white-label to RIAs. (Comparable: Personal Capital → ~$1B Empower acquisition.)
- **Betterment Premium fee increase** — human-CFP tier from **0.40% → 0.65%** ($100k min); base robo is 0.25%. **Kitces:** 0.65% is almost exactly the 0.70% average independent-advisor yield — affirmation that human advice is worth paying for.
- **LPL + Trust & Will** — LPL (22,000 advisors) partners for estate **document prep** (wills/POAs/directives/revocable trusts), distinct from estate *tax* planning (Vanilla/Luminary/FP Alpha). Doc prep is the far larger market (99% of clients vs. <1% with estate-tax exposure).
- **[AI] SEC "AI-washing" enforcement** — warned against claiming AI use when not actually doing so; **fined 2 firms a combined $400,000** for "false and misleading statements about their use of artificial intelligence" (one claimed to be the "first regulated AI financial advisor").
- **SEC off-channel fines** — **$81M** against 16 BD/RIA platforms (Feb 2024); LPL **$50M** settlement (March 2024). Compliant texting: MyRepChat, Redtail Speak.
- **Cerulli/Fidelity research:** tech-savvy advisors win on **back-office efficiency** (e-sign, CRM, video, money movement, trading), not client-facing portals.

### April 2024
- **Orion price increase: +8.9%** (effective Apr 1, even outside renewal cycles), blaming "extreme inflation." **InvestCloud** followed with **+5%**. (Redtail had raised prices the prior year.) **Kitces:** drives inquiries to Panoramix, Advyzon, Wealthbox.
- **Orion Estate tool** (extension of Orion Planning) — workflows for estate info collection, asset-flow flowcharts, What-If scenarios. **Kitces:** mismatch — Orion Planning has low-single-digit adoption and its users are the least planning-centric.
- **[AI] Vanilla "Scenarios"** — models UHNW estate strategies (GRATs, SLATs, CRUTs, ILITs) and projects estate-tax savings under growth/TCJA-sunset hypotheticals (replacing Leimberg's NumberCruncher). Only ~250,000 US households have >$25M net worth.
- **Wealthfeed** raised **$2M** — prospecting on local money-in-motion (new movers, marriages, capital raises, liquidity events); CRM cross-reference.
- **SmartAsset AMP** (Advisor Marketing Platform) — automated lead nurture, auto call/text, and "live connections" (SmartAsset gets the prospect on the phone first). Typical successful conversion ~5% (20/3/1).
- **[AI] Shaping Wealth "Lydia"** (with Alai Studios) — AI conversational agent that **simulates a client** so advisors can practice difficult behavioral-finance conversations (not a client-facing advice bot).

- **Salesforce FSC + BridgeFT "Attune"** — pre-built multi-custodian data feeds on AppExchange for upper-mid RIAs ($500M-$2B). Salesforce has >50% share among 10+-advisor firms, ~12% among 3-or-fewer, ~17% overall.
- **[AI] SEI invests $10M in TIFIN** — focused on AI for wealth management; TIFIN pilots new AI with SEI advisors first. (TIFIN: $109M Series D at $842M valuation early 2022, then layoffs and leadership turnover.)
- **Nebo Wealth + Advyzon Investment Management** — packaged trading for Nebo's liability-driven-investing approach (turning Nebo into a TAMP/SMA front end).
- **MSCI acquires Fabric Risk** (founded 2019; institutional-grade risk analytics on MSCI data) — to use as a proprietary sales/illustration tool. Little advisor adoption pre-acquisition.
- **[AI] Prospecting category formalized on the AdvisorTech Map:** **Catchlight** (ranks leads by advisor-specified factors, pre-writes outreach), **AIdentified** (scrubs imported contacts, real-time event alerts), **FINNY** (finds prospects in contacts + learns from past clients), **Wealthawk** (pre-qualifies + scans clients for money-in-motion), **Equilar** (finds intros to high-value targets via network).

## Regulatory timeline

- **Early 2021** — **SEC Marketing Rule** updated for the first time in ~60 years, allowing advisors to solicit/use client **testimonials** (spawned Wealthtender, IndyFin, Testimonial IQ, and later the Hadrius AI testimonial scanner). *(Pre-2022 context; drives 2022-2024 review-site activity.)*
- **March 2022** — **SEC proposed Cybersecurity Rule (Rule 206(4)-9)** for RIAs: risk assessments, access/info-protection policies, incident response; Rule 204-2 recordkeeping of incidents; Rule 204-6 disclosure of "significant" cyber incidents on Form ADV Part 2A ("cybershaming"). Commissioner Allison Lee cited data compromises up ~70% in two years.
- **June 2022** — **DoL PTE 2020-02** rollover-advice compliance took effect (June 30), driving tools like RIA In A Box + InvestorCOM's RolloverAnalyzer.
- **July 2022** — **Charles Schwab $187M SEC settlement** for misleading investors about "free" Schwab Intelligent Portfolios (undisclosed cash-allocation revenue/drag): $45.9M disgorgement + $5.6M interest + $135M penalty into a Fair Fund.
- **April 2023** — **Betterment $9M SEC settlement** for tax-loss-harvesting misrepresentations (2016-2019: scanned every other day not daily; coding errors; different behavior with third-party models). ~25,000 of 275,000 opted-in accounts; ~$4M client benefit lost.
- **July 2023** — **SEC proposed the Predictive Data Analytics (PDA) rule** — would require advisers/BDs using "covered technology" that predicts/guides investor behavior to **eliminate or neutralize conflicts of interest**. Definition so broad it could capture rebalancers, risk-tolerance tools, and planning software. Heavy industry pushback (IAA, Investor Advisory Committee asked to narrow scope). SEC sent letters to RIAs requesting detail on AI/algorithmic use and AI marketing claims.
- **Sept 2023** — **Massachusetts Secretary Galvin** sent AI-use investigation letters to **JPMorgan Chase, Morgan Stanley, Tradier Brokerage, US Tiger Securities, Savvy Advisors, and Hearsay Systems.**
- **Oct 2023** — **Fidelity cut off screen-scrapers** (Oct 1), forcing aggregators onto its direct **Akoya** API; **CFPB monitoring** the arrangement for anti-competitive potential (broader CFPB screen-scraping/open-banking rulemaking in the background).
- **2021-2024 (cumulative) — "Off-channel" communications enforcement:** SEC fines for unarchived texts/WhatsApp reached ~**$2.7B cumulatively** across the industry since 2021. Notable 2024 actions: **$81M across 16 firms (Feb 2024)** and **LPL's separate $50M settlement (March 2024)**. (Drove Archive Intel, MyRepChat, Redtail Speak, CurrentClient, MessageWatcher.)
- **Jan 2024** — Ongoing PDA-rule debate; outcome seen as tied to the 2024 election (SEC leadership is appointed).
- **March 2024** — **SEC's first "AI-washing" enforcement:** fined **two firms a combined $400,000** for "false and misleading statements about their use of artificial intelligence" — including one claiming to be the "first regulated AI financial advisor." Paired with a broader SEC warning against AI-washing.
- **July 2024** — **State-level credential-sharing crackdown widens:** after Washington's late-2023 action specifically against Pontera, **Missouri** sent warning letters to ~45 state-registered RIAs about credential-sharing tools generally.
- **Oct 2024** — **Fidelity moved to block credential-sharing platforms** (Pontera-style) from acting in its 401(k) accounts (>$3.4T DC assets, 30M participants) — a private-market echo of the regulatory pressure.

**Kitces' throughline on AI regulation:** the SEC's posture (broad PDA rule + AI-washing enforcement + state AI inquiries) pushes AI *away* from advice-giving/client-facing roles and *toward* back-office productivity — reinforcing the market reality that the AI tools advisors actually adopted (notetakers, document scanners, writing aids) were support tools, not advice engines.
