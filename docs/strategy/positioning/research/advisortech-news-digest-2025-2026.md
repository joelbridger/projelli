# AdvisorTech News Digest: 2025–2026
*Source: Kitces "Latest in #AdvisorTech" monthly roundup series*
*Compiled: June 2026 | Coverage: January 2025 – June 2026*

## Section 1: Chronological Highlights (Newest First)

### June 2026

- **Altruist** plans corporate RIA launch — compliance, E&O, tech support for independent IARs as 1099 contractors; first custodian to offer this model; higher payouts possible since custody economics are more favorable than traditional W-2 payout grids
- **Flourish Lending** launches mortgage platform (via Sora Finance acquisition) — ~0.5% below-market mortgage rates by bypassing the retail originator layer; integrates into Flourish cash management platform
- **TaxStatus** + **Advice.ai** integration **[AI]**: curated library of tax planning strategies (built and vetted by humans); AI matches IRS-verified client data to applicable strategies; generates ranked list of strategies with estimated savings; CEO Kevin Knull co-founded both companies; IRS-direct data gives accuracy others lack
- **RISR** launches AI document reading for business owner niche **[AI]**: buy-sell agreements, business tax returns, insurance policies for business owners; targets planning complexity CPA firms handle
- **Janus Henderson** client survey (1,000 high-net-worth investors): 40% feel good about AI for administrative/educational content; only 24% for investment recommendations; 40% are upset if advisors use AI to automatically respond to client texts or emails; 80% are upset if advisor uses AI without disclosing it — disclosure is table stakes
- **"Vibe coding"** tools (Replit, Bolt, Canva Code) producing MORE niche AdvisorTech point solutions, not fewer — Fingale (projection reporting), MySSAgent (Social Security), Leveridge (payroll optimization), PocketFiling (estate/trust tax), OpAlpha (rebalancing overlays) as recent examples; Kitces: AI-lowered build costs mean the map keeps growing, not consolidating

### May 2026

- **Morningstar sells ByAllAccounts** to Pello Companies (startup incubator) — originally acquired 2014 for $28M; follows Envestnet selling Yodlee (Jul 2025); CFPB's Open Banking Rule being rolled back under Trump; Kitces: account aggregation is systemically broken and is being abandoned by the major players
- **FINNY** launches "Hunter" **[AI]**: combined AI Chief Growth Officer — both outbound prospecting and inbound content creation/marketing campaigns in one tool; expanding from outbound-only with $17M Series A raised (Jan 2026); 400 firms, ~$6,000/year each
- **Advisor360** embeds **Conquest Planning** inside its platform (bundled subscription, additional fee) — adds financial planning engine to Advisor360's "Unified Data Fabric" to enable AI agents to run planning scenarios on unified data **[AI]**; requires advisors to switch planning software, which is a significant barrier
- **Wealth.com** raises $65M Series B led by Schwab — expanding from estate planning into tax; plans to use Ester AI development capital and acquisitions to expand into additional specialized planning categories
- **CurrentClient** raises $1.25M seed — unified advisor phone line + compliant texting; plans AI call screening, transcripts, and CRM integration **[AI]**
- **RIAs building in-house AI**: Savant Wealth plans $15–20M/year for 3 years ("bionic agents"); Carson Group deployed in-house chatbot "Steve" (real estate–like playbook); Kitces: off-the-shelf tools at $500–$1,200/seat vs. $50M+ in-house builds — only massive RIAs can justify in-house

### April 2026

- **Wealthbox** launches AI Assistant + AI Agent **[AI]**: natural language queries and actions against CRM data; "playbooks" for frequently-used prompts; AI Agent monitors CRM daily and surfaces client touchpoint suggestions (birthday follow-ups, upcoming reviews); Kitces notes it is slow compared to newer AI-native tools; Redtail still has no announced AI tools
- **Jump** expands to "AI Operating System" with three distinct modules **[AI]**:
  - Meet module: meeting notetaker (original product)
  - Grow module: meeting scorecards for training, "Signals" that auto-detect held-away asset mentions and referral sources in meeting transcripts, tracks advisor emotional intelligence index
  - Operate module: client intake forms, document extraction, email drafting
  - Pricing dropped from $120 base to $100 base; individual features spun off as add-ons
- **RightCapital SmartImport** launches **[AI]**: AI reads uploaded investment statements, meeting notes, PDFs of competitor financial plans (eMoney, MoneyGuide), and auto-populates the RightCapital financial plan; bundled free with all tiers — bad news for standalone extraction tools Powder and Flextract
- **Range** reiterates plan to "eliminate human advisors" — built human advisor team as AI training data, now "flipping a switch" to AI-only model; Kitces skeptical: clients paying for human-advisor fees will leave without the human; net-negative AUM event likely
- **WealthStream** "Advice Intelligence" platform **[AI]**: trains newer advisors on firm's planning philosophy; AI surfaces recommended strategies from client data and maps them to firm curriculum; positioned as coaching tool, not client-facing AI; acquired by Envestnet
- **Equity compensation tools** proliferating: Gemifi, Trayecto, Grantd (now with StockOpter), EquityNav all active in the space

### March 2026

- **Altruist launches Hazel tax planning add-on** **[AI]**: AI-generated tax analysis for custodial clients — triggers a stock market selloff across RIA custodians (Schwab -12%, LPL -22%, Raymond James -10%); Kitces: real story is Altruist's continued emergence as a legitimate custodial competitor, not just a startup anymore
- **Jump raises $80M Series B** (Insight Partners) — largest funding round ever for an advisor AI notetaker; positions Jump as long-term "advisor OS" player
- **Zocks raises $45M Series B** — signals notetaker space is a real category with major VC conviction; both Jump and Zocks now claim they're building full advisor platforms, not just note tools
- **Jump claims 27,000 advisor users** — approximately 10% of all U.S. financial advisors; fastest-growing software adoption rate Kitces has tracked
- **FMG Suite acquires Testimonial iQ** (rebranded FMG Testimonials) — AI-structured testimonial capture and compliance workflow for RIA marketing
- **Tax planning commoditization** wave: Wealth.com, Nitrogen, Altruist Hazel, Hive Tax AI, and april all launch tax planning tools; Kitces: 92% of advisors now do some tax planning, 50%+ use Holistiplan; tax is becoming table stakes, not a differentiator
- **Contio / MeetingOS** (Aaron Klein, Riskalyze founder) launches **[AI]**: AI-generated pre-meeting agenda pulled from CRM data; real-time talking points during meeting; post-meeting notes; Kitces warns: more information surfaced during meetings may create information overload and slow advisor-client connection
- **Max** (MaxMyInterest rebranded) launches private banking for UHNW RIA clients (high-yield deposits, institutional loans)

### February 2026

- **Pershing** launches RIA client referral program ($50k/year minimum + 30bps on referred AUM) — latest custodian to compete for RIA referral flow; follows Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood, Goldman Sachs
- **iAlta acquires BridgeFT** — Envestnet co-founder Bill Crager's new venture; building "Envestnet for alternatives" using BridgeFT's custodial data connections to bridge alternatives infrastructure into advisor workflows
- **TaxStatus** launches Tax Return History report: side-by-side comparison of up to 4 years of IRS-pulled tax returns — shows income trend, deduction shifts, year-over-year changes
- **AI notetaker meeting analytics** highlighted: Jump and Zocks both track advisor talk-time vs. client talk-time through meetings, client sentiment trends, and Advisor Emotional Intelligence Index scores — useful for training but Kitces questions whether it genuinely drives revenue
- **Hubly** integrates with Jump, GReminders, Pulse360 — shows that AI tools need structured workflows to be useful; workflows themselves don't need to be replaced by AI (and shouldn't be)

### January 2026

- **FINNY raises $17M Series A at $150M valuation** — 400 firms, ~$2.4M revenue = 62.5x revenue multiple; only ~5–7% of advisors do meaningful outbound prospecting; Kitces: is the market large enough at scale?
- **Charles Schwab shuts down Intelligent Portfolios Premium** — hybrid robo + CFP access, $30/month; proves the "missing middle" (cheap + human access) is not a viable model; neither true robo nor true human service
- **Nevis raises $35M at $200M valuation** (Sequoia, Ribbit, Iconiq) — AI admin automation for advisors; Kitces: Kitces Research shows integration capabilities and AI admin automation don't drive revenue productivity; advisors who use tech for "better advice quality" are 2x more productive than those using it for efficiency
- **Morningstar + PitchBook apps inside ChatGPT** — subscribers access proprietary investment research data directly inside ChatGPT; bad for standalone AI investment research startups (Brightwave, Fiscal.ai, Boosted.ai, ARQA)
- **Zeplyn** pivots to "agentic AI" (Agent Nexus) **[AI]**: as AI notetaker market saturates, Zeplyn repositions as a chatbot interface for CRM data, email history, and uploaded documents; can draft personalized email responses to common client questions; Kitces: it's really a search interface with good UX, not a true autonomous agent
- **"AI data layer" race begins**: Advisor360 (Unified Data Fabric + AI agents), Orion (Denali AI), Dispatch (data orchestration), Milemarker Navigator all competing to be the unified intelligence layer sitting on top of all advisor tech stacks **[AI]**

### December 2025

- **Vanilla** launches "Starter" tier ($99–$199/month for basic estate document templates, no complex planning support) — making estate planning accessible to advisors who don't want full Vanilla subscription
- **FP Alpha NextGen Tax Insights** **[AI]**: AI matches uploaded client tax data (tax return + manual inputs) against a curated library of advisor-vetted strategies (backdoor Roth, 1031 exchanges, S-corp elections, Qualified Opportunity Zone, charitable strategies, etc.); AI generates reasoning for why each strategy applies; strategies are built and vetted by human CPAs, NOT generated by AI — positions itself on trust/accuracy vs. open-ended AI
- **WealthReach** launches — de-anonymizes website visitor data to identify prospects by firm and revenue; Kitces: most advisors don't generate enough site traffic to benefit ($10k+ investment needed)
- **Altruist Hazel** pulls in custodial data **[AI]**: AI notetaker now integrates CRM + email + calendar + Altruist custodial platform data; positions Hazel as a cross-system intelligence layer rather than just a meeting tool; raises question of whether AI notetakers will eventually replace CRMs
- **Mobile Assistant** considered selling 5M+ meeting transcripts for AI training but declined; later acquired by Jump (November), data deleted — important privacy signal for advisors
- **Alternatives infrastructure boom**: Morgan Stanley acquires EquityZen; Schwab acquires Forge; GeoWealth launches private market model marketplace — alts going mainstream in RIA space

### November 2025

- **RightCapital** launches data migration tool **[AI]**: OCR scans PDF eMoney financial plan outputs and auto-populates RightCapital plans; positions as easy switching tool; eMoney had quietly built a similar tool for the reverse direction for about a year
- **Advyzon** announces in-house financial planning software launching in 2026 — hired ex-MoneyGuide executives; all-in-one CRM+portfolio management+planning would be a significant integration story
- **Elements** (planning-light tool) — foreclosed on by creditors after raising $9M; $299/month price point never found product-market fit; Kitces: not enough planning depth to replace full financial planning, not cheap enough to be impulse purchase
- **Hamachi** launches **[AI]**: AI email drafting trained on firm's fund/investment materials and investment philosophy; drafts client responses to investment questions; includes compliance review built in; founded by former Orion and Redtail founders (Eric Clarke, Brian McLaughlin)
- **Jump acquires Mobile Assistant** — human transcription service; rebrands as Jump Pro and markets to Mobile Assistant's existing advisor customers; Mobile Assistant shuts down November 20, 2025
- **Dispatch raises $18M Series A** — "data orchestration" (not data warehousing): syncs advisor data across tech platforms in near-real-time without storing it all centrally; Kitches: addresses integration pain without the data concentration risk

### October 2025

- **Origin** launches as "AI financial advisor" **[AI]**: SEC-registered; budgeting + brokerage + financial planning projections + AI recommendations in one app; $1/year first year, $99/year thereafter; targets DIY investors, not replacing human advisors yet; Kitces: consumer client acquisition cost remains the unsolved problem
- **Grantd acquires StockOpter** — AI-native startup (equity compensation planning) acquires legacy tool to gain user base and content library
- **RedBlack** rebrands and relaunches as standalone rebalancer — spun from Intelliflo/Invesco, sold to Carlyle; fills gap left by consolidation
- **AI notetaker price compression** arrives: Altruist Hazel and Wealthbox both priced at ~$50/month; Jump, Zocks, Finmate AI all introduce lower-cost basic tiers ($67–$76/month); Kitces: platforms embedding notetakers are forcing standalone tools to reduce prices or add value elsewhere
- **Slant** launches as AI-native "relationship CRM" **[AI]**: AI captures "memories" from client notes and surfaces them proactively before calls/meetings; generates advisor "nudges" (follow-up reminders, relationship cues, sentiment-driven alerts); integrated meeting notetaker at launch; $150/month; Kitces: currently lacks deep integrations with financial planning software — needs those to be a real CRM replacement

### September 2025

- **RightCapital** launches RightExpress — topic-specific plans for prospecting (Social Security optimization, retirement readiness, debt elimination, risk analysis); each deliverable is a narrow output from a subset of financial planning data; designed for prospect engagement without full onboarding
- **Altruist One** launches: subscription bundle at 12bps/year; includes zero mutual fund transaction fees, better cash sweep (4.25% APY), tax-loss harvesting, discounted access to Model Marketplace; Kitces: 12bps is competitive but not fee-only; cash rate better than most competitors
- **TaxStatus** launches Enterprise LeadGen platform: advisors pay to access prospect IRS-verified tax data after prospect provides authorization; also identifies which prospects would benefit most from the advisor's services
- **FMG Suite** acquired by GTCR (private equity) — fifth acquisition in advisor digital marketing; Kitces: PE rollup strategy for advisor tech content/marketing
- **FINNY** launches Intent Search **[AI]**: identifies individual prospects who've recently searched financial topics online (retirement, estate planning, fiduciary); auto-generates targeted outreach tied to what they've been researching

### August 2025

- **Nitrogen** launches "AI Meeting Center" **[AI]**: native AI notetaker built into the proposal/risk tolerance platform; competitive response to Jump/Zocks; all meeting notes stay inside the Nitrogen platform where risk data already lives
- **Kitces Research on Advisor Technology 2025** (major findings): integration quality = biggest driver of advisor satisfaction with their tech stack; BUT integration does NOT drive revenue productivity; advisors using tech to "provide better advice quality" are 2x more productive than those using it for "reduce cost/save time"; efficiency and quality are different use cases
- **GReminders** launches "Do Anything" AI Agent **[AI]**: pre/post-meeting automation via natural language commands; Kitces: it is sophisticated workflow automation, NOT true autonomous AI agency — the distinction matters for advisor trust in the tool
- **Anthropic** launches Claude for Financial Services **[AI]**: enterprise tier; integrates with FactSet, Morningstar, and S&P Global data; gives hedge funds and sovereign wealth funds a financial-research-trained LLM; directly threatens standalone AI investment research startups (Brightwave, Fiscal.ai, Boosted.ai, ARQA)
- **EncorEstate Plans** + eLegacy partnership: state-specific attorney review add-on for $200; 16 states covered; advisor-designed estate plan plus licensed attorney review for liability coverage
- **LPL** deploys AI to help advisors understand payout grids; **Cambridge Investment Research** deploys AI to reduce new account opening from 9 days to 17 minutes

## Section 3: Signals Most Relevant to Advisor Prep Hero

*Advisor Prep Hero: local-first AI that reads across an advisor's files, email, and tools, and answers questions with citations.*

---

### 1. AI Document Reading Is Becoming the Central Competition

The entire industry is racing toward AI that reads client documents and extracts structured data:

- **Tax returns**: Holistiplan (dominant, 50%+ adoption), FP Alpha Tax Module, RightCapital Tax Analyzer, TaxStatus Financial Baseline Report, RightCapital SmartImport (reads competitor plans too)
- **Estate documents**: Wealth.com Ester AI (executive summary with citation links), Vanilla AI Copilot (summary + diagram + chat Q&A), FP Alpha estate module
- **Investment statements**: RightCapital SmartImport reads uploaded statements and auto-populates the financial plan
- **Business documents**: RISR (buy-sell agreements, business tax returns, commercial insurance) — Jun 2026
- **Meeting notes**: Jump Operate module adds document extraction as a feature alongside notetaking

**What this means for Advisor Prep Hero:** Every planning category is getting its own specialized extraction tool. Advisor Prep Hero's advantage is reading *across all of them at once* and answering questions that require connecting information from multiple documents. A tool that tells you what the tax return says is different from a tool that connects the tax return to the estate plan to the investment statement and surfaces the implication.

---

### 2. The "AI Data Layer" War is Advisor Prep Hero's Exact Market

Five companies are now explicitly competing to be the cross-source AI intelligence layer for advisors:

- **Advisor360 Unified Data Fabric**: all client data sources → AI agents run queries
- **Orion Denali AI**: advisor OS unifying Orion's sprawling acquired systems
- **Dispatch** ($18M Series A, Nov 2025): data orchestration that syncs across systems without centralizing
- **Milemarker Navigator**: AI layer for large RIAs managing multi-platform tech stacks
- **Jump "AI Operating System"** (Apr 2026): Grow + Operate modules are explicitly building the same cross-system intelligence layer

**What this means for Advisor Prep Hero:** The "AI layer on top of advisor data" is the recognized battleground. Advisor Prep Hero's differentiation must be: (a) local/private vs. cloud-centralized, (b) reads private files and email (not just connected platforms), and (c) answers with citations the advisor can verify. The data layer framing validates the market and the architecture.

---

### 3. Citations and Verifiability Are the Trust Signal

Multiple tools have discovered that advisors won't trust AI-generated summaries unless the AI shows its work:

- **Wealth.com**: "executive summary with internal citation links" — the citations are the selling point
- **FP Alpha NextGen Tax Insights**: strategies from a curated advisor-vetted library with reasoning shown; Kitces explicitly praises this vs. open-ended AI
- **Vanilla AI Copilot**: chat function lets advisor ask about specific document clauses (verifiable by source)
- **TaxStatus**: IRS-direct data (not AI inference) is the trust anchor
- **Zeplyn Agent Nexus**: positions its value as "you can see which documents it drew from"

**Janus Henderson client survey** (Jun 2026): 33% of HNW clients are upset about AI-generated investment recommendations, 80% are upset if advisors use AI without disclosing it. Trust, transparency, and verifiability are not optional differentiators — they are table stakes that the whole market is now working to address.

**What this means for Advisor Prep Hero:** The citation-first approach is proven right by market behavior. Every tool that advisors trust is showing its work. Advisor Prep Hero's "answers with citations" framing should be front and center, not a technical detail.

---

### 4. Privacy and Data Control Are Active Concerns

- **Mobile Assistant** (Dec 2025): Nearly sold 5 million+ advisor-client meeting transcripts for AI model training. Decided not to, but the advisor community learned this was even considered. Jump acquired the company and deleted the data.
- **Janus Henderson survey** (Jun 2026): 80% of HNW clients upset if advisor uses AI without disclosure
- **Mili** (Feb 2025): Raised $2M positioning primarily on security/privacy for meeting notes. Kitces said it was the wrong differentiator — but by Dec 2025, the Mobile Assistant near-miss proved privacy concerns are real, not just marketing noise.

**What this means for Advisor Prep Hero:** "Local-first" and "your data never leaves your machine" is a genuine product advantage, not just a positioning preference. The Mobile Assistant situation is a concrete example to cite when explaining why local AI matters for advisor-client relationships.

### 5. CRMs Adding AI — But Slowly and Inadequately

- **Wealthbox**: announced Project Althea in April 2025 (AI notetaker), launched fall 2025; added AI Assistant + AI Agent in April 2026 — two years behind Jump; Kitces says it's slow vs. AI-native tools
- **Redtail**: no announced AI tools as of April 2026 (the largest advisor CRM by user count)
- **Advisor360**: acquired Parrot AI (Feb 2025) and embedded; has Tandem CRM with "Unified Data Fabric" ambitions
- **Slant** (Oct 2025): AI-native "relationship CRM" launches at $150/month — explicitly built from scratch for AI, not retrofitted; lacks financial planning software integrations

**Kitces Research finding** (Aug 2025): integration quality is the #1 driver of advisor satisfaction with their tech stack — BUT it does NOT drive revenue productivity. The market is paying for integrations that don't move the needle.

**What this means for Advisor Prep Hero:** The traditional CRM layer is slow to add real AI. Advisors who want AI intelligence need it NOW, from a tool that isn't waiting for Redtail or Wealthbox to build it. Advisor Prep Hero can run alongside the CRM the advisor already has without requiring them to switch.

### 6. Meeting Notetakers Are Expanding Beyond Meetings

The trajectory: meeting notes → CRM sync → planning software sync → document extraction → proactive intelligence → full advisor operating system.

- **Jump** (Apr 2026): Operate module adds client intake forms, document extraction, email drafting — no longer just meeting notes
- **Zeplyn** (Jan 2026): pivots to "agentic AI" that queries historical CRM data, email history, and uploaded documents — same cross-source intelligence ambition as Advisor Prep Hero
- **Altruist Hazel** (Dec 2025): adds custodial data; questions whether AI notetakers will eventually replace CRMs
- **Slant** (Oct 2025): AI-native CRM builds the notetaker in from the start
- **Finmate AI** (Jun 2025): pushes meeting data to financial planning software, not just CRM

**What this means for Advisor Prep Hero:** Jump and Zeplyn are building toward Advisor Prep Hero's territory from the "meeting notes" entry point. Advisor Prep Hero's entry point is different: the private file vault and email. Both arrive at cross-source client intelligence, but from different angles. The question is which entry point creates more defensible value and trust.

### 7. AI-Native CRM Is an Emerging Threat Category

### 8. Open-Ended AI Generation Is Being Rejected — Curated Libraries Win

- **FP Alpha NextGen Tax Insights**: curated strategy library built by human CPAs; AI matches, doesn't generate; Kitces praises this explicitly
- **TaxStatus + Advice.ai**: same curated-library approach for IRS-verified data matching
- **Conquest Planning SAM**: AI selects from a preset strategy menu, doesn't generate novel strategies
- **Janus Henderson survey**: clients are comfortable with AI for admin but deeply uncomfortable with AI-generated investment recommendations (only 24% comfortable)

**Anthropic Claude for Financial Services** (Aug 2025): even enterprise-tier AI for investment research is grounded in FactSet/Morningstar/S&P data — proprietary verified data, not open-ended generation.

**What this means for Advisor Prep Hero:** "Answering questions with citations from the advisor's own files" is the right product architecture. The market is validating citation-grounded, retrieval-based AI over open-ended generation. Advisor Prep Hero should not try to generate recommendations — it should surface what the advisor already has and show exactly where it came from.

### 9. Account Aggregation Is Collapsing — A Gap Advisor Prep Hero Can Fill

- Envestnet sells Yodlee (Jul 2025) — originally paid $590M in 2015; sold for ~$100–$220M
- Morningstar sells ByAllAccounts (May 2026) — originally paid $28M in 2014; sold to startup incubator
- CFPB Open Banking Rule being rolled back under Trump administration

The infrastructure for aggregating *financial data* from bank/brokerage feeds is declining. But the infrastructure for reading *private advisor documents* (tax returns, estate plans, investment statements the client brought in) is actively growing.

**What this means for Advisor Prep Hero:** Account aggregation isn't Advisor Prep Hero's battle. Document intelligence is. The collapse of aggregation infrastructure doesn't hurt Advisor Prep Hero — it validates reading the documents the advisor already has rather than relying on API connections that may not survive.

### 10. Microsoft / Major Platform Risk Is Real But Not Immediate

- **Anthropic Claude for Financial Services** (Aug 2025): enterprise tier is aimed at hedge funds and sovereign wealth — NOT the advisor market yet

- **Vanguard, LPL, Cambridge** all building custom AI internally, not buying it

**What this means for Advisor Prep Hero:** Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT plugins are moving toward proprietary financial data integration. This affects investment research tools more than practice management tools for now. But a future where Microsoft 365 Copilot reads all advisor email and documents — and advisors already use Outlook + OneDrive — is a real long-term risk. Local-first and Reg S-P compliance are the natural counters.

**What this means for Advisor Prep Hero:** Don't lean into "agentic AI" language. The market is starting to see through it. "AI that reads your files and answers questions" is more honest and more useful. Advisor Prep Hero's value proposition is information retrieval and synthesis with citations — that's what advisors actually want, and calling it "agentic" would invite the same Kitces skepticism.
