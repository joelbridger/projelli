Co-Founder Agreement
May 2026  ·  Version 1.0
Parties to this agreement
Jameson Samuel Daines Founder — referred to in this document as "Jameson"
Matthan William Daines Incoming Co-Founder — referred to as "Matthan"
1 What This Agreement Is

This is the agreement between Jameson and Matthan about how they will work together on Keepance, how money gets split, and what happens if either of them decides to stop.

Both parties agree to everything written below by signing at the bottom.

2 What Matthan Will Do

Matthan joins Keepance as Co-Founder. His primary responsibilities include:

  • Marketing: running launch campaigns, social media, community outreach
  • Market research: understanding what customers want and what competitors are doing
  • Outreach: contacting newsletters, directories, podcasts, and communities
  • General co-founder input: contributing ideas, feedback, and direction on the product and business

This role can grow over time by mutual agreement between Jameson and Matthan.

3 Matthan's Ownership of the Company
Matthan owns 25% of Keepance. This is not handed over all at once — he earns it gradually over 4 years.
First 6 months No ownership vests yet. If either party stops within this window, Matthan receives no ownership. This is called the cliff.
At 6 months Matthan earns 12.5% of his total share all at once (equal to 3.125% of the total company).
Months 7 – 48 The remaining ownership trickles in each month automatically, in equal monthly amounts.
After 4 full years Matthan has earned his complete 25%.
Example: If Matthan stops after 2 years, he has earned roughly half of his 25%, which is about 12.5% of the company. The other half returns to Jameson.

Keepance is currently a sole proprietorship. This ownership agreement is binding between both parties, but to make the 25% fully official and legally protected, Keepance should be converted to an LLC or corporation within 12 months of signing. Both parties commit to doing that together.

4 Matthan's Cut of Every Sale
Until Keepance has made $25,000 in total cumulative sales, Matthan receives 50% of every sale.

Every time a customer buys Keepance, Matthan gets half of what they paid, and Jameson keeps the other half. This applies to every pricing tier.

How sales are tracked: Total cumulative sales are measured using the gross sale amount in the LemonSqueezy dashboard (the amount the customer paid, before platform fees). Matthan has read-only access to the LemonSqueezy dashboard at all times.

When Matthan gets paid: Jameson pays Matthan his share within 15 days of the end of each calendar month.

5 What Happens When Sales Hit $25,000
🎉 Milestone
When total cumulative sales reach $25,000, two things happen automatically.

First: The 50% sales cut stops. From that point on, Matthan earns money through his 25% ownership only — not through a cut of individual sales.

Second: Both Jameson and Matthan sit down together within 30 days of hitting $25,000 to renegotiate. This conversation covers:

  • Matthan's ongoing role — whether it stays focused on marketing, or expands into other areas of the business
  • Whether a new revenue share makes sense at the new scale of the company
  • Whether a salary or regular payment should be introduced
  • Any changes to responsibilities as the product grows

Reaching $25,000 in sales is a milestone worth celebrating. Both parties are obligated to show up to the renegotiation conversation in good faith and complete it within 30 days of hitting the milestone.

6 Everything Built for Keepance Belongs to Keepance

Anything Matthan creates while working on Keepance belongs to Keepance, not to Matthan personally. This includes:

  • Marketing copy, ads, and campaign materials
  • Social media accounts created for Keepance
  • Email lists and contact databases built for Keepance
  • Research documents, competitive analyses, and strategy documents
  • Any designs, videos, or creative assets made for Keepance

If Matthan ever steps away from Keepance, he does not take any of these things with him. They stay with the company.

7 Keeping Things Private

Both Jameson and Matthan agree to keep the following things private, and not share them with anyone outside of Keepance without the other's permission:

  • How much money Keepance makes
  • The customer list and any customer data
  • The product roadmap and future plans
  • Any business strategies discussed between them
  • Pricing details and business decisions

This confidentiality continues even after either party stops working on Keepance.

8 Not Working for a Competitor

While working on Keepance, and for 12 months after stopping, Matthan agrees not to build, co-found, or work in a marketing role for a product that directly competes with Keepance. A competing product means a local-first AI workspace desktop app targeting indie founders.

This clause is narrow on purpose. It does not stop Matthan from having other jobs, doing other work, or working in marketing in any other industry.

9 Decision Making
Matthan decides

All day-to-day marketing decisions. He is trusted to execute without needing approval.

Jameson decides

Product direction, pricing changes, major business strategy, and any single spend over $300.

If there is ever a disagreement that cannot be resolved, both parties agree to talk it through before taking any formal action. Family comes first.

10 If Either Person Wants to Stop
If Matthan wants to stop
  • He gives Jameson 30 days notice
  • He keeps whatever ownership he has already earned
  • The 50% sales cut stops on his last day
  • Any unearned ownership returns to Jameson
If Jameson asks Matthan to stop
  • Jameson gives Matthan 30 days notice
  • Matthan keeps whatever ownership he has already earned
  • Matthan receives a final sales payment for sales already in progress
If Keepance is sold
  • Matthan receives his ownership percentage of the sale price, based on how much he has vested at that time
Example: If Keepance sells for $400,000 and Matthan has earned his full 25%, he receives $100,000.
11 What This Agreement Does Not Cover

This agreement does not cover salary. Neither Jameson nor Matthan is paying the other a regular wage right now. The 50% sales cut is Matthan's only regular income from Keepance until ownership becomes valuable. Both parties understand and accept this.

If either party wants to introduce a salary in the future, that would be a separate conversation and a separate written agreement.

12 This Is a Binding Agreement

Both parties have read this agreement, understand it, and agree to it. Both parties also agree to have this document reviewed by a lawyer within 60 days of signing, and to update it if a lawyer identifies anything that needs to change.
