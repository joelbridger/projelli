# Advisor Prep Hero Design Partner Program (Brief)

**Status:** DRAFT for Jameson's review. Recruiting materials for the 3.0 design-partner firms. The outreach messages are in `outreach-templates.md`.

> This brief has two uses: it tells you (Jameson) what the program is, and the "What it is" through "What we ask" sections can be shared, lightly edited, with an interested attorney after the first reply.

---

## Why this program exists

Advisor Prep Hero 3.0 is being rebuilt around what real attorneys actually need: find anything you have ever emailed or filed in seconds, privately; draft and redline in real Word with tracked changes; and never hand a client's data to an AI vendor. Before it ships, I want a small group of practicing attorneys shaping it on real work, so 3.0 is built for their day instead of my guesses. They also become the proof that other lawyers will trust ("a lawyer I'd have a drink with uses this"), which the research says is the single biggest thing that moves an attorney from interested to adopting.

Recruiting these firms is the one launch-critical task that cannot be automated. It should start now, in parallel with the build.

## Who we are looking for (5 to 10 firms)

- Solo and small-firm attorneys (2 to 10 lawyers), with a lean toward litigators, since their pain (depositions, discovery, "what did the client say") is deepest.
- People who live in Outlook and Word all day and feel the "I can't find anything in my email" pain.
- AI-curious but privilege-conscious: they want the leverage and refuse to risk client confidentiality. (This tension is the whole point.)
- A spread across practice areas (litigation, transactional, a little IP if possible) and email providers (mostly Microsoft 365, some Gmail).
- Decision-makers about their own tools.

The detailed screen is the research screener at `~/keepance/docs/research/2026-06-08-ux-attorney-v2.5.1/instruments/screener.md`.

## What a design partner gets

- A direct line to me and real influence over what 3.0 becomes.
- Early access to the email-search and the in-Word redlining before anyone else.
- Founding pricing locked for good, and white-glove setup on their own machine.
- Optional recognition as a founding advisor, and, if they want it, a CLE or a written piece we do together. Entirely their call.

## What we ask

- A handful of short conversations across the build (an intro call, then occasional feedback sessions). Light, spread out, stop anytime.
- Trying it on their own work. During any recorded session we use test or non-confidential material only, never live client data on a call.
- Honest reactions, including the unflattering ones. The unflattering ones are the most useful.
- If, and only if, they would genuinely recommend it, a short testimonial at the end.

## The confidentiality answer (their first question)

- Their files and their clients' data stay on their machine. I never see them, and neither does any Advisor Prep Hero server.
- They can run the AI fully local (nothing leaves the computer at all), or use their own AI account directly, with a clear indicator showing exactly where any request goes.
- I will show them, in plain language they can repeat to a worried client, exactly where their data lives and who can see it.
- They are never locked in. Their documents are real Word and PDF files in their own folders.

## Commitment and exit

Light, spread over the build, and they can leave whenever they like with no obligation. This is a partnership to make the product right, not a contract.

## The ask in one line

A 20-minute intro call to see if it is a fit, then we take it from there.
