# Design Partner Outreach (DRAFT for Jameson to review and send)

These are written in your voice: first person, plain, no hype, short, and respectful of an attorney's time. They do not overclaim, because the research is clear that lawyers shut off the moment something sounds like marketing. Edit names and the one personal line in brackets, then send. Nothing here sends automatically.

A note on channel: email or a LinkedIn message both work. Keep it short either way. The goal of the first message is a 20-minute call, nothing more.

---

## Template A: warm (someone you know, or a referral)

**Subject:** a favor, and an early look

[First name],

I'm building a tool for lawyers and I'd value your eyes on it before it's finished.

The short version: it lets you search your entire email and document history in plain English and actually find the thing, it drafts and redlines in Word with real tracked changes, and your client data never leaves your computer or touches an AI company's servers. That last part is the whole reason I'm building it.

I'm putting together a small group of attorneys to shape it on real work and keep me honest. Light commitment, a few conversations over the next stretch, and you'd get it free with founding pricing locked after. [Personal line: I thought of you because you've complained about exactly this / because you live in your email like I do.]

Worth a 20-minute call? No pitch, I mostly want to watch how you work and hear what's wrong with it.

Thanks either way,
Jameson

## Template B: cold or lightly-referred (a litigator you don't know well)

**Subject:** a private AI tool for lawyers, looking for a few to shape it

[First name],

I'll be quick. I'm a product designer building software for attorneys, and I'm looking for a handful of practicing lawyers to help shape it before launch.

It does two things lawyers keep telling me they need. First, it finds anything in your email and files instantly, the stuff Outlook search never surfaces. Second, it drafts and redlines in Word, with tracked changes, so the output is a real document and not a copy-paste mess. And it does both without sending a single client file to an AI company. Everything stays on your machine, and you can run it fully offline if you want.

I'm not selling anything. I want five to ten attorneys who'll try it on real work and tell me, bluntly, what's useful and what's broken. In return you get early access, founding pricing locked for good, and a direct line to me.

Could I have 20 minutes to show you and hear how you actually work? If it's not for you, no hard feelings.

Best,
Jameson

## Reply bank (for the questions you'll get)

**"Is my client data safe?"**
Yes, and that's the point. Your files and email stay on your computer. I never see them and neither does any server of mine. You can run the AI fully local so nothing leaves the machine at all, or use your own AI account directly, and either way there's an indicator showing exactly where a request goes. I'll walk you through it so you could explain it to a client.

**"How much time is this?"**
Light. A 20-minute intro, then occasional short sessions across the build. You set the pace and can stop anytime.

**"What does it cost me?"**
Nothing during the program, and founding pricing locked for good after. The only ongoing cost is the AI usage you'd pay your provider directly, usually a few dollars a month, and I take no cut of that.

**"What do I get out of it?"**
Early access to the email search and the in-Word redlining, real influence over the product, founding pricing, and, if you want it, recognition as a founding advisor or a CLE we do together. Your call.

**"Do I have to use real client files?"**
Not on a call with me. We use test or non-confidential material whenever I'm watching. On your own time, you can point it at whatever you like, since it all stays on your machine.

**"What kind of lawyer are you looking for?"**
Solo and small firms, especially folks who live in email and Word and have wanted to use AI but won't risk confidentiality. Litigators especially, but not only.
