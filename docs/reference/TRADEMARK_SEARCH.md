## Caveats

- This is an **informal clearance**, not a formal legal opinion. A trademark attorney's full search would also check:
  - State trademark registers (50 states)
  - Common-law uses (unregistered marks)
  - International trademark databases (WIPO, EU IPO)
  - Phonetic similars (not just exact matches)
  - Visually similar marks
- Risk of conflict is **low but non-zero**. The most likely conflict scenario would be an unregistered common-law use somewhere — typically a small business operating under a similar name in a related field.
- Mitigation: file the USPTO application early in Month 2 of revenue to establish constructive notice and lock in priority date.
