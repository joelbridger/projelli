P6 Meetings on the client Wave 3

The full meeting experience, in its decided home: a Meetings tab in the client’s own tab row (the sidebar stays three tabs). P7 Book view & whole-practice Ask Wave 4

Two quiet lenses on what you already have. The Clients home gains a “Whole book” toggle that ranks clients neediest-first, with a completeness meter, staleness, and the specific gaps to chase. Ask gains a permanent scope pill: switch to “Whole practice” and it answers from each client’s summary only (client chips with cited passages), never breaking client isolation.
