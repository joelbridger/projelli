Notes for Jameson

  1. The calendar is one card among peers, not a new section. It sits in the same Connections list as Microsoft 365, IMAP, OneDrive, Wealthbox and the rest, styled identically. No new nav item, no settings page: connecting a calendar adds exactly one card.
  2. The three provider choices live inside the card, not a wizard. Connect Microsoft, Connect Google, or paste a calendar link (.ics) are all right there on the card. There is no multi-step flow to walk through.
  3. “Read-only” is the trust signal. Both states say it plainly: it reads your calendar to match meetings, and never creates, edits, or deletes events. Connected shows the honest sync window (“past 7 days + next 14”) and a last-synced line.
