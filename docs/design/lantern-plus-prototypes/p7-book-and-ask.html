Notes for Jameson

  1. Book view is a lens, not a new place. It is a second view of the Clients home you already have (the same “Clients / Whole book” toggle you use elsewhere), reading the completeness the app already tracks per client. No new tab, no dashboard, no charts. Try the toggle: “Clients” is your familiar list, “Whole book” just re-sorts it by who needs you most.
  2. Whole-practice answers never break client isolation. When you ask across the whole book, the app reads only each client's short saved summary, never their raw documents. That promise is written right into the answer (“summaries only”), and the results are plain client chips with the exact cited line, never a spreadsheet of everyone's files.
  3. The scope pill means you always know what the AI can see. One pill, always visible, spelling out the current scope and its size (“the Hendersons · 214 files · 891 emails · 6 meetings”). One click switches it, and switching to “Whole practice” changes both what is searched and how the answer is shown.
