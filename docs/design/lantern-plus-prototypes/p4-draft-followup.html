Notes for Jameson

  1. One modal is the whole feature. “Draft follow-up” sits beside Export in the Word toolbar, styled like its neighbors so it does not shout. Clicking it opens a single modal. There is no second screen and no wizard. If it needed one, we designed it wrong.
  2. Save to my Drafts is the primary action, not Send. The advisor always sends from their own inbox, so the emphasized button drops the draft into their Outlook. Send is there as the secondary choice for when they are ready. The AI never sends on its own.
  3. The recipient is yours, and every claim is traceable. The To field is prefilled from your email history but stays editable, so the AI never silently picks who gets the email. The three citation chips tie each promise back to the exact line in your notes. Hover one to see it.
