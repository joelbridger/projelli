## How to use this list

### Triage order

1. **HIGH priority first.** These 14 candidates have the strongest overlap between what they publicly care about and what Projelli does. Reply rates on warm-ish DMs with real personalization tend to be 20-40% in this tier.
2. **MED priority next.** 16 candidates who fit the ICP but are either noisier, more saturated with pitches, or a looser thematic fit.
3. **LOW priority last.** 6 candidates who are long shots but worth a DM because the upside (a signal boost from them) is large if they say yes.

### DM send cadence

- **No more than 8 DMs in a single day.** If 4 of them reply, you need time to have a real conversation with each. If none reply, you've learned something about the pitch and want to iterate before sending 20 more copies.
- **Space HIGH-priority DMs ~30 minutes apart** so if someone replies fast you can respond inside 5 minutes.
- **Run X DMs between 9-11 AM and 1-3 PM (their local time when visible).** Those are the two windows where indie X users are most likely to read and reply same-day.

### Follow-up rule

- **One follow-up after 5 business days, max.** One-line nudge, not a re-pitch. "Hey, circling back in case the last one got buried. No worries if not your thing." After that, leave them alone.
- **If they reply positively**, reply inside 5 minutes if humanly possible. Send the download link and license key in that reply, not in a separate message.
- **If they say no**, thank them and ask if there's someone in their circle who fits. Half the best beta testers come from referrals, not cold outreach.

### Tracking

Copy the CSV block at the bottom of this file into a Google Sheet or Airtable. Update `dm_sent`, `replied`, `installed`, `feedback` as each candidate moves through the funnel. The sheet is the source of truth, not your memory.

### Voice reminder

Every DM below has been checked against the no-em-dash / no-banned-words rules. If you rewrite one before sending, run your edit through the same filter. The banned list: `leverage, seamless, empower, unlock, delve, tapestry, elevate, transform, journey, cutting-edge, robust`. No em dashes. First-person singular. Contractions. One specific concrete reference to something they did or said.
