# Keepance Marketing Vision — Private Client Intelligence

**Status:** Canonical marketing vision as of 2026-06-22. Approved by Jameson.
**Supersedes:** the older "local-first AI workspace for confidential work" / "private intelligence layer for a law practice" framing as the *lead* category.

## The one-line shift

Keepance is not "private AI search." Keepance is **private client intelligence**.

That is the difference between *"ask questions about files"* and *"understand this client, matter, or household well enough to help the professional serve them."*

## The category

> **Private client intelligence layer.**

Keepance does not replace Clio, Outlook, Word, a CRM, eMoney, or a financial-planning tool. It sits across them and turns a firm's scattered client knowledge into something searchable, structured, and reusable.

## The clean messaging system

- **Category:** Private client intelligence layer.
- **Primary promise:** Securely ask questions across your files and emails, with citations you can verify.
- **Differentiator:** A living **Client Map** for every client, matter, or household, built from documents, notes, and guided interviews.
- **Outcome:** Better advice, faster onboarding, fewer missed details, stronger continuity.
- **Trust proof:** Local-first, cited answers, a client-controlled data path, human review.

## The deeper pain (the "no-brainer" frame)

The pain is not "I can't find my files." The deeper pain is: **the context that makes us good at serving clients is scattered across emails, documents, notes, meetings, and people's heads.**

- **Before Keepance:** client knowledge is scattered, fragile, and trapped in memory. New staff need hand-holding. Old context gets missed. Important facts hide in email. Client nuance disappears between meetings.
- **After Keepance:** each client has a living map. The firm can ask questions, verify sources, see what's missing, prepare for meetings, onboard staff, create notes, and serve with continuity.

## The "facts vs people" framing

> **Most AI tools help you find facts. Keepance helps you understand clients.**

Strongest headline form: **Generic AI knows the world. Keepance knows the client.**

Other variations: "Chatbots answer questions. Keepance understands the client file." / "Search finds the document. Keepance explains the client."

## Named product concepts (build the brand around these)

1. **Client Map** — the core product object: the structured, living profile of a client, family, matter, or household. May include: client story, key people, relationships, goals, risks, open loops, timeline, preferences, communication style, sensitive issues, documents, prior advice, important emails, missing information, firm-specific guidance, and next best questions.
2. **Context Completeness** — a score/report of what you know, what you're assuming, and what you still need to ask. Copy: *"See what you know, what you assume, and what you still need to ask."* Frames Keepance as risk-reduction and quality-of-service, not just productivity.
3. **Guided Client Interview** — copy: *"Keepance walks you through the questions that turn a folder of documents into a full client picture."* (Advisor variant: Household Discovery Interview. Lawyer variant: Matter Discovery Interview.)
4. **Firm Philosophy** — copy: *"Keepance stores your firm's philosophy and applies it as context."* Avoid "learns" (professional audiences distrust unsupervised AI). This makes Keepance act like the firm, not generic AI.

## Hero / value-prop variations (explorable in the tab tool)

These are the full value-prop versions to choose among. The live tab explorer at `website/landing-options.html` renders each in the real brand design.

**Best core value prop:** Keepance is a secure tool that indexes your files and emails and answers questions about them using AI. Then it turns each client, matter, or household into a living Client Map (history, relationships, goals, risks, preferences, missing context, and your firm's way of serving them) so your team can solve that client's specific problems without starting from scratch.

**Sharper (likely favorite):** Keepance is a secure AI workspace that indexes your files and emails, answers questions with citations, and builds a living Client Map for every client or household. It helps your team understand the people behind the paperwork (their history, goals, risks, open questions, and context) so every answer, note, draft, and handoff is specific to them.

**Premium / strategic:** Keepance turns your firm's scattered client knowledge (emails, files, notes, and interviews) into private client intelligence. Ask questions, find cited answers, see what you still need to learn, and give every client advice shaped by their actual life, matter, family, and history.

**Punchy:** Keepance gives your firm a private AI memory for every client. It indexes your files and emails, maps each client's full context, and helps your team answer, draft, prepare, and hand off work with the details that matter.

**Lawyer-specific (use "matter"):** Keepance is a private AI workspace that indexes every matter file, email, transcript, and note, then answers questions with citations you can verify. For each client or matter, it builds a living Client Map (facts, parties, timeline, risks, open questions, preferences, and firm strategy) so attorneys and staff can understand the case faster and act with context.

**Advisor-specific (use "household"):** Keepance is a private AI workspace that indexes client files, emails, plans, notes, and reviews, then answers questions with citations you can verify. For every household, it builds a living Client Map (family relationships, goals, accounts, estate concerns, tax context, risk tolerance, open questions, and service preferences) so advisors can prepare, document, and serve with the full picture.

## Audience notes

- **Lawyers:** use **matter** more than **family**, unless targeting estate planning, family law, elder law, or private-client practices. Backdrop: ABA Formal Opinion 512 (duties of competence, confidentiality, communication, client protection when using generative AI).
- **Advisors:** **household** is the magic word (captures spouses, children, trusts, estates, taxes, goals, accounts, family dynamics). Backdrop: SEC Regulation S-P amendments (data handling, incident response, safeguards, service-provider oversight).
- **All:** investment advisers especially need accurate, supervised AI claims (accuracy, oversight, confidentiality, vendor terms, recordkeeping, no overstated AI capabilities).

## Positioning sentence for sales calls

Keepance is for firms where the most valuable client knowledge is scattered across documents, emails, notes, and people's heads. It turns that knowledge into a private, cited, structured Client Map so the whole team can understand the client and act with context.
