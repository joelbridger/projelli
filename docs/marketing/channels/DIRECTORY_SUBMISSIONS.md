## 1. BetaList

**Submit at:** https://betalist.com/submit

**Startup name:** Projelli

**URL:** https://projelli.com

**Tagline:** The AI workspace that writes to your hard drive, not the cloud.

**Description:**
Projelli is a desktop app for indie founders who want to use AI without losing control of their work. Every conversation with Claude, GPT, or Gemini gets saved as a real Markdown file in a folder you pick — no proprietary format, no vendor lock-in, no subscription to a cloud database you don't own.

It ships with 15 founder-specific workflow templates: Pricing Strategy, Pitch Deck Outline, GTM Plan, MVP Scope, Competitive Analysis, and more. You bring your own API key, so you pay the model providers directly and Projelli takes nothing per-query.

Built on Tauri (Rust + React), it runs on Windows, Mac, and Linux. One-time pricing: $29 Founder's Launch for the first 100 buyers, then $49 Pro or $99 Lifetime. No monthly fee, ever.

**Maker's comment:**
I kept starting AI conversations in ChatGPT or Claude.ai that actually mattered — product strategy sessions, pricing teardowns, pitch prep — and then losing them. They'd disappear into a feed, or I'd forget which tab they were in, or the interface would get rebuilt and the history would vanish.

I wanted those conversations to live as plain files next to the rest of my work. So I built Projelli. It's a local-first workspace where every AI session is just a Markdown file on your drive — readable anywhere, searchable, backupable however you want. I'm an indie founder building this for other indie founders who've had the same frustration.
