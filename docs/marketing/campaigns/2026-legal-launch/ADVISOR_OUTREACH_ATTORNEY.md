## Version A: Warm intro (you know them or have a mutual)

**Subject:** Quick ask about some legal workflow templates

Hey [Name],

I'm Jameson. [One sentence of how you know them / who connected you.]

I'm building a desktop app called Advisor Prep Hero, and I think I need about an hour of your time, honestly.

The short version: it's a local-first AI workspace for attorneys and other professionals who can't pipe their work into ChatGPT. Everything stays on your machine. Your API keys go direct from your computer to the provider and never touch a server I control. Basically the anti-cloud version of Notion AI.

I'm building a template pack specifically for solo and small-firm attorneys: a Deposition Contradiction Finder, a Privilege Log Drafter, a Case Timeline Builder, a Discovery Document Triage tool, a Client Intake Synthesizer, and a few others. I have decent product instincts, but I'm genuinely not a lawyer, and I'd rather have someone who actually practices look at these before I ship them to attorneys who will immediately know if I got something wrong.

What I'm asking for is pretty narrow. Seven templates, probably 20-30 minutes to read through, a "this is credible / here's what's off" pass. Not legal advice, not a formal review. Just: does this reflect how you actually work?

If that sounds interesting, I'd love to jump on a 15-minute call first so you can see the product before deciding. No pressure either way.

Thanks for reading.
Jameson Daines
keepance.com

Jameson
keepance.com

## Notes for sending

- Follow up once after 1 week if no response. One follow-up only.
- If they ask "what's in it for me": credit in the app, early free access to the Professional tier, and being named as a technical reviewer on the website (optional, their call).
- Do not offer equity or payment for an advisory review. This is a product feedback relationship, not a formal advisory board role.
- If they're a partner at a larger firm (10+ attorneys), they are probably not the right fit. You want someone who actually does the day-to-day work themselves.
