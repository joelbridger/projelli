# Design-partner recruiting copy

**Owner: Jameson.** One person at a time, personal, never a blast. Open every message with a real detail about them. Goal: 5-8 partners who do confidential client work, can be named, and will actually use it.

Voice rules apply (no em dashes, first person, contractions). These are honest local-first pitches. No compliance claims.

---

## Warm contact (someone Jameson already knows)

> Hey [name], I've been building something for the last few months and you're exactly the person I want using it before I make any noise about it.
>
> It's called Keepance. It's a local-first AI workspace: you chat with Claude or GPT inside it, and every conversation becomes a real document in a folder on your own machine. Nothing routes through my servers, and you bring your own API key. I built it for the kind of work you can't really paste into someone else's cloud, which is basically your whole job.
>
> I'm putting together a small group of people who actually do this work to use it for real and tell me what's wrong with it. You'd get the full app free, a direct line to me, and real say over what ships next. The only thing I want back is honest use on real work and your honest read. If it turns out genuinely useful, I'd love to quote you on the site, but that's only if it earns it, no pressure either way.
>
> Want in? I'll get you set up today.
>
> Jameson

## ICP cold-ish (a solo attorney / CPA / consultant you don't know well)

Use when reaching someone in the network-of-a-network, or a practitioner who's posted about distrusting cloud AI tools.

> Hi [name], I saw [the specific thing: your post about not trusting AI with client files / your practice / the referral from X]. I'm building a tool for exactly that problem and I'd value your eyes on it.
>
> Keepance is an AI workspace that runs on your own machine. You chat with an AI and every conversation is saved as a plain file in a folder you control. Your API key stays in your keychain, the requests go straight to the provider, and nothing of yours ever touches a server of mine. For confidential client work, that's the whole point.
>
> I'm looking for a few practitioners to use it on real work and tell me honestly where it falls short. Full app, free, no clock. Direct line to me. If it earns it, I'd ask to quote you, with your name or just your role, whichever you're comfortable with.
>
> Worth a look? I'll send you the download and get you going.
>
> Jameson

## The "I already resist cloud tools" crowd (LocalLLaMA / privacy / IndieHackers)

For someone who already runs local models or refuses cloud AI. Lead with the part they care about.

> Hey [name], you clearly already care about keeping your data on your own machine, so this is aimed right at you.
>
> I built a desktop AI workspace where every chat saves to disk as plain Markdown, your key lives in your keychain, and you can run the whole thing against a local Ollama model so nothing leaves the box at all. No account, no telemetry, no server of mine in the path.
>
> I'm getting a small group of people who actually live this way to put it through real use and tear it apart. Free license, direct line to me, real influence on the roadmap. Honest feedback is all I want back, and a quote later only if it's actually good.
>
> Up for it?
>
> Jameson

## After they say yes (onboarding note)

> Awesome, thank you. Here's the download: https://keepance.com (grab the trial build, and I'll flip your license to a comped one so there's no clock). When you first open it, pick your profession on the welcome screen and it'll set your workspace up with the right starting templates. Try running one real thing through it this week, something you'd actually do for a client, and tell me where it annoyed you. The annoyances are the most useful thing you can give me.

## Notes

- Comp the license as soon as they're in. Don't make a design partner sit behind a trial clock.
- The testimonial ask is a *separate, later* message, only after they've gotten real value. Use `../2026-06-first-dollar/TESTIMONIAL_ASK.md`. Don't ask on day one.
- Track each partner: who, vertical, date onboarded, last check-in, testimonial status + permission. A simple list is fine; just keep it durable.
- If someone gives great feedback but the product isn't for them, that's still a win. Thank them, write it down, move on. Not every partner becomes a testimonial, and that's expected.
