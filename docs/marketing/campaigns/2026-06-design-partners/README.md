# Design-Partner Engine: 2026-06

**Why this exists.** The single biggest conversion blocker right now is **zero social proof**. Our ICP (solo attorneys, CPAs, independent consultants) is the most risk-averse buyer alive, and nobody wants to be the first stranger to put privileged work into an unknown tool. One real customer breaks that wall; **2-3 named testimonials unlock the loud launch** (Ambrogi / Above the Law / Product Hunt). This program is how we manufacture that proof deliberately instead of waiting for it.

This is the companion to the first-dollar sprint (`../2026-06-first-dollar/`). First-dollar is about *a sale*. This is about *a named reference* who actually uses Keepance for real work. They overlap, and that's the point: the first buyers are the best design-partner pool.

## The program in one paragraph

Recruit **5-8 design partners** who do exactly the work Keepance is for. Give them the full app free (a comped license), a direct line to the founder, and real influence over what ships. In return, ask for two things: they actually use it on real work for a few weeks, and if it's genuinely useful, a short testimonial we can attribute by name and role. No money changes hands. The deal is access + influence for honest use + (if earned) a public reference.

## Who to target (in priority order)

1. **Warm network first.** Anyone Jameson already knows who does confidential client work: ex-colleagues now consulting, a friend who's a solo attorney or CPA, an agency owner. Warm + ICP + free = the highest-yield testimonials, and the lowest-friction asks.
2. **The "local-first already" crowd.** Privacy-conscious solos who already resist cloud tools (the r/LocalLLaMA, r/privacy, IndieHackers folks the first-dollar posts reach). They convert on the honest pitch and they write detailed feedback.
3. **One credible name per vertical.** A single recognizable solo attorney, one CPA/EA, one independent strategy consultant. A named quote from a real practitioner in each vertical is worth more than ten anonymous five-star lines, and it's exactly what the law/tax launch needs.

Hard rule: a real first name + real role beats a glowing anonymous quote every time. "Independent strategy consultant, [first name]" is the asset. Aim the recruiting at people who *can* be named.

## The offer (what they get)

- The full app, comped (a free license, no trial clock).
- A direct line to the founder. Their feedback actually changes the product.
- First look at new profession packs, and a say in what's in them.
- Founding-member framing: they were here before anyone.

## The ask (what we want back)

- Use it on **real work** for ~2-3 weeks. Not a demo poke, actual client files.
- Honest feedback, especially the parts that frustrate them.
- If (and only if) it earns it: a short testimonial we can attribute by name + role, and the okay to use it on the site.

## Cadence

1. **Recruit** with the copy in `OUTREACH.md` (one person at a time, personal).
2. **Onboard:** comp their license, make sure they get to a real first-run workspace. Point them at one workflow that fits their job so they hit a magic moment fast.
3. **Check in** after about a week. Ask what's working and what's annoying. Write it down; the annoyances are the roadmap.
4. **Ask for the testimonial** only after they've gotten real value (use the script in `../2026-06-first-dollar/TESTIMONIAL_ASK.md`, don't duplicate it). Always get explicit, specific permission for the name + the wording.

## How this feeds the bigger launch

Two or three named testimonials from real practitioners is the gate between "honest local-first pitch to early adopters" and "loud law/tax launch to a skeptical professional audience." The moment we have them:
- They go on the homepage and the vertical pages (replacing the current zero social proof).
- They become the credibility hook in the Ambrogi / Above the Law / Product Hunt pitches (drafts already in `../2026-legal-launch/`).
- The advisor recruiting and the testimonial recruiting reinforce each other: a practicing attorney who reviews a pack is also a candidate to be named as a reference.

## Honesty guardrails

- Comped license, but the testimonial is never part of the deal. They give it only if the product earns it. A bought review is worse than no review.
- No compliance claims in any of this. Local-first + BYOK + chat-as-files is the true story; keep it there until advisors sign off on the statutory framing.
- Log every testimonial and its written permission somewhere durable. A quote on the site without documented permission is a liability, not proof.
