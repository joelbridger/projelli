# Journey Beyond Wealth: flagship design partner (warm, via family)

**Why this one matters.** It's the only warm path into the ICP we have, and it's a good one. Financial advisors (RIAs) handle some of the most sensitive client data there is (full financial pictures, SSNs, account numbers, estate plans), they're bound by SEC Reg S-P and a fiduciary duty, and they are exactly as nervous about feeding client data into cloud AI as any lawyer. Local-first + BYOK lands hard here. Journey Beyond Wealth is reachable through a real personal connection ([your wife's name] is a client), so it skips the cold-start problem entirely.

**What we want from it, in order:**
1. One advisor actually using Advisor Prep Hero on real work for a few weeks.
2. The first **named testimonial** from a real practitioner.
3. Validation (or not) of a **financial-advisor vertical** we hadn't been targeting. If it resonates, that's a fourth pack and a fourth landing page down the line.

**The honest framing:** we don't have a financial-advisor pack yet. That's fine. Lead with the general local-first app (Personal tier) and the confidentiality story, and be upfront that the advisor-specific templates would be shaped partly by their feedback. Do not make regulatory claims (no "Reg S-P compliant"). The true, sufficient pitch is "your client data never leaves your machine, you bring your own AI key, and every chat becomes a real file you own."

**Routing (Jameson's call):** the warmest path is [your wife's name] mentioning it to her advisor and offering to connect them, or forwarding the short note below. The direct path is Jameson emailing the advisor referencing the relationship. Warm-via-[your wife's name] is softer and higher-yield; use it if she's comfortable. Either way, keep it low-pressure: this is a family relationship first.

## After they're in

- Comp the license immediately (no trial clock for a design partner).
- Point them at the workflows closest to advisor work: the general templates plus the consulting pack (Client Discovery Synthesizer, Confidential Research Memo, Stakeholder Map). Note the gap honestly, and ask what an advisor-specific pack would need.
- Check in after about a week. The friction they hit is the roadmap.
- Testimonial ask is a separate, later message (use `TESTIMONIAL_ASK.md`), only once they've gotten real value, and always with explicit permission for the name + wording.

## The bigger bet

If Journey Beyond Wealth resonates, financial advisors become a real fourth vertical: a confidentiality-anxious, well-paying, software-buying audience (they already pay for eMoney, RightCapital, Redtail, etc.), with the same local-first wedge that the legal and tax stories use. One enthusiastic advisor reference is the seed for that whole line. Treat this relationship accordingly: it's worth more than its single seat.
