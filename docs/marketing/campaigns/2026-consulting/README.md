# 2026 Consulting Campaign

> **Goal:** Establish Advisor Prep Hero as the tool for independent strategy consultants and boutique agencies
> who work under NDA and can't use cloud AI with client work.
>
> **Timing:** Post-legal and post-tax campaigns. This is the "viral edge" audience — they post about
> their tools, which accelerates word-of-mouth into law and tax.
>
> **No gating dependency on an advisor.** Consulting NDA use cases don't require the same statutory
> verification as legal/tax. Can start outreach earlier once the Consulting Practice pack is built.

## Channel priority order

1. **Umbrex** — concentrated community of independent MBB alumni consultants, self-selected high-WTP
2. **Lenny's Racquet** — product/strategy audience, overlaps with consultants and PMs
3. **Tom Critchlow's newsletter** (The Strategic Independent) — independent consultants specifically
4. **r/consulting** — Reddit, broad reach, lower intent but good for feedback
5. **Substack cross-promo** — writers who write about tools, knowledge work, or consulting

## The Consulting Practice pack (v2.3) — 5 templates

1. Client Discovery Synthesizer — turn discovery call notes into a structured brief
2. Confidential Research Memo — NDA-safe research output for client delivery
3. Stakeholder Map Generator — map decision-makers, influencers, and blockers
4. NDA-Safe Slide Outliner — structure a client presentation before writing it
5. Engagement Retrospective Builder — close-of-engagement review and lessons learned

## Key marketing angles

- **NDA clause proliferation:** More client contracts now include explicit AI prohibitions. Local-first
  sidesteps those clauses entirely because client data never leaves your machine.
- **Client confidentiality as a differentiator:** Consultants who can credibly say "your information
  never leaves my machine" have a competitive advantage in pitching sensitive engagements.
- **The "I don't want to explain this to the client" problem:** Even when AI use isn't prohibited,
  many consultants don't want to have the conversation about what they used with client data. Local-first
  makes that conversation unnecessary.
