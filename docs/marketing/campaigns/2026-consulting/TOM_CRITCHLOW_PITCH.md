## Proposed guest post

**Title:** The NDA clause your next engagement letter will probably have

**Abstract (200 words):**

Independent consultants have spent the last two years figuring out which AI tools fit their workflow. That question is becoming more complicated. A new clause is appearing in engagement letters across M&A advisory, private equity due diligence, and high-value strategy engagements: explicit restrictions on AI tool use with client materials.

The clause language varies. Some versions prohibit using AI tools that "retain, store, or train on" client information. Others name specific platforms. A few are drafted broadly enough to cover any cloud-connected AI tool where the data flow isn't fully documented. Most consultants I've talked to have signed at least one agreement with this language in the last year without quite realizing it was there.

This piece is a practical guide for independent consultants. It covers: what these clauses actually say, why they're appearing now, how to identify them in your own agreements, and what your technical options are when a client's engagement letter restricts your AI tool use. It also argues that getting ahead of this is actually a competitive positioning move, not just a compliance task. Consultants who can say "yes, I can meet your AI data requirements" are going to have a cleaner sales conversation than those who can't.

The goal is to give consultants a clear framework before this becomes a negotiating standard. It's already a trend. It hasn't quite landed yet in most indie consulting communities.
