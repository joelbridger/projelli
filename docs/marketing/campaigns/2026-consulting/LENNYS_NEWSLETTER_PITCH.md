# Lenny's Racquet Pitch

> **Lenny's Racquet:** lennysnewsletter.com — Lenny Rachitsky's newsletter and podcast.
> 750K+ subscribers. Audience is product managers, startup operators, and consultants.
> Paid placement exists but editorial placements ("Lenny recommends") are more valuable.
>
> **How to reach:** Lenny is reachable via his newsletter, Twitter/X (@lennysan), and LinkedIn.
> He is genuinely hands-on with tool recommendations. The audience leans product-builder but
> includes a strong independent consultant and fractional executive segment.
>
> **When to pitch:** After Consulting Practice pack ships. Have a clear demo ready.

---

## Cold pitch to Lenny (Twitter/X DM or email)

Hey Lenny,

I built a tool I think is genuinely useful for the consultant and operator segment of your audience — the people who are doing client work under NDA and are nervous about cloud AI touching that material.

It's called Keepance. Local-first AI workspace: your files stay on your machine, your API key goes direct from your computer to the provider. Nothing passes through a server I control. For anyone doing sensitive client work, that matters.

It has a Consulting Practice template pack: Client Discovery Synthesizer, Stakeholder Map, Confidential Research Memo, Slide Outliner, Engagement Retrospective. These are the actual workflows independent consultants run on engagements, not generic templates.

Happy to give you a demo or send a trial license. No pitch, just showing you the product.

Jameson
keepance.com

## Lenny's "Tools I Use" or "Reader Toolkit" angle

If Lenny or his team uses the product and finds it valuable, the strongest outcome is an organic mention in one of his tool roundups or a reader Q&A where Keepance comes up naturally.

Alternatively: sponsor the newsletter for one issue targeted at the consultant/operator segment. Lenny's newsletter ad rates are publicly disclosed (~$X per issue for the email slot). If traction from other channels is solid, this is worth evaluating.

---

## Notes

- Lenny's audience skews toward people building software, not practicing law or accounting. The consulting angle fits better here than the legal or tax angles.
- If the product has 50+ paying customers by the time you pitch Lenny, lead with that social proof. If not, lead with the product story and a clear use case.
- The AI-confidentiality angle may resonate with PMs and operators who are doing vendor work or enterprise consulting where their NDAs are strict.
