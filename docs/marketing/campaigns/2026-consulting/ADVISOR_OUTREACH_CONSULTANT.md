# Advisor Outreach — Independent Consultant

> **Send when:** Consulting Practice pack is ready to build (post-tax campaign, ~Q4 2026).
> Target: independent strategy or management consultant with their own practice,
> ideally someone who works on sensitive client engagements and has strong opinions
> about tools.

---

## The email

**Subject:** Building an AI tool for independent consultants — would you review some workflow templates?

Hey [Name],

I'm Jameson Daines. I'm a product designer and I've been building a desktop app called Advisor Prep Hero for consultants and other professionals who work under NDA and can't always use cloud AI with client material.

The core thing: everything stays on your machine. Your client files, your API keys, your research notes. Nothing goes through a server I control. I built it partly because the "your clients' data in our AI" conversation is one I'd rather not have to have, and I figured other people felt the same way.

I'm putting together a Consulting Practice template pack: a Client Discovery Synthesizer, a Stakeholder Map Generator, a Confidential Research Memo template, an NDA-Safe Slide Outliner, and an Engagement Retrospective Builder. They're built around how I think independent consultants work, but I'd rather have someone who actually runs client engagements read them before I ship.

The ask is small: five templates, maybe 20 minutes to read, a "this makes sense / this is how it would actually get used" pass. I'd love to show you the app in a 15-minute call first.

If you're open to it, let me know.

Jameson
keepance.com

- LinkedIn: search "independent management consultant" or "fractional strategy" with active posting history
- Your own network — consultants you know from design or product work who've gone independent

## Notes

- No statutory verification needed for consulting (unlike legal/tax), so you don't need a formal advisor sign-off before using marketing copy about NDA protection.
- The NDA angle is the strongest one — "your client data never leaves your machine" resonates with consultants who've had the awkward conversation with a client about AI use.
- If they ask what's in it for them: same as attorney/CPA advisors. Credit in the app, early free access, optional public acknowledgment.
