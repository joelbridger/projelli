# Projelli Marketing Strategy

_Created: 2026-04-27 by Claude (CEO mode), ratified by Jameson Daines (board)_
_Re-review: monthly through month 6, then quarterly_

This is the strategic spine of Projelli's marketing for the next 12 months. The eight documents in this folder are the plan. The channel-level execution playbooks in `../channels/`, `../playbook/`, and `../action-packs/` are the tactics. **Read this README first, then `00-master-strategy.md`, then whichever sub-doc matches the work you're about to do.**

---

## The plan in one paragraph

Projelli's goal for the next 12 months is **$10,000 in monthly gross revenue by month 12**, sustained for 60 consecutive days, alongside Jameson's full-time job at Wheel Health, inside a 5-10 hour-per-week budget. The strategy runs two engines in parallel: a **compounding SEO + AI-search engine** that turns a one-time investment of 30-40 high-quality pages into 12+ months of free qualified traffic, and a **spike booster engine** of launch beats and partnerships that fuel the first engine and harvest revenue at moments of attention. Personal brand binding follows a **selective hybrid** model: Projelli has its own voice on `@projelli` for daily marketing, while Jameson amplifies 1-2 times per month from his real-name accounts in the 5% project-mention slot of his personal brand strategy. Pricing is locked at $0 / $49 / $99 with a $29 Founder's Lifetime tier capped at the first 100 buyers. ICP is locked at indie founders / building-in-public solopreneurs. We do not pivot, verticalize, add subscriptions, or chase enterprise. We measure monthly, retrospect quarterly, and abort or redirect on seven explicit triggers.

## The 1-page summary

### Definition of winning
$10,000 monthly gross revenue by month 12, sustained 60 days, inside 5-10 hr/week.

### ICP
Indie founders, building-in-public indie hackers, solo developers shipping software products. Privacy-leaning, cost-leaning, local-tool-leaning, AI-native.

### Positioning (canonical one-liner)
**Obsidian for the AI era, built for founders, sold once.**

### Five message pillars
1. **Local-first**: your data stays on your machine
2. **BYOK**: you bring your own AI key, we never see your data
3. **Chat-as-files**: every conversation becomes a real Markdown file
4. **Founder-specific**: 15 templates for the work founders actually do
5. **Sold once**: $49 Pro / $99 Lifetime, no subscription

### Pricing
$0 free / $49 Pro one-time / $99 Lifetime one-time / $29 Founder's Lifetime (first 100 buyers)

### The two engines
| Engine | Job | Investment | Decay |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engine 1: SEO + AI search** | Compounding qualified traffic | 30-40 pages over 12 months, ~3-5 hr/wk Q1 then ~1-2 hr/wk | None (compounds) |
| **Engine 2: Launch beats + partnerships** | Spike traffic that fuels Engine 1 | Launch week + 5-7 newsletter sponsorships + 3-5 integrations + 3-5 podcast spots | 7-30 days per spike |

### The selective hybrid
- `@projelli` brand account: daily marketing, replies, threads, blog
- Jameson real-name accounts: 1-2 Projelli posts per month, in the 5% slot of personal brand
- Press kit: founder bio with real name + photo
- Wheel Health firewall: never reference internal work, never use Wheel resources for Projelli

### What we will not do
1. No paid ads in year one
2. No founder face on the marketing site
3. No subscription tier
4. No collaboration features
5. No AI margin (BYOK forever)
6. No category-leadership PR push
7. No "Projelli for X" verticalization
8. No public roadmap commitments

### Cadence
- **Weekly review**: 15 min, Sunday
- **Monthly review**: 60 min, first Monday
- **Quarterly retrospective**: 2 hr, end of quarter

### Abort triggers
1. Revenue 50% under plan at month 3
2. Single channel >70% of revenue for 2 months
3. Refund rate >12% for 2 months
4. Zero AI-search citations at month 9
5. Wheel Health expresses concern (kill switch)
6. Legal threat (consult counsel)
7. Jameson hours <5/wk for 4+ weeks

### Year-1 outcome bet
- 600-1,200 paying customers
- 15K-25K monthly organic visitors by month 12
- 2,000-5,000 email list
- 100+ referring domains
- 8-12 page-1 rankings on priority queries
- 4-6 AI-search citations on priority queries

## How this doc was made

This strategy was authored on 2026-04-27 in a single CEO-mode session. The board (Jameson) picked the definition of winning (revenue-focused, hands-off) and the personal brand binding mode (selective hybrid). Claude proposed three strategic approaches, the board approved the recommended hybrid (Approach 1 primary + Approach 2 secondary), and Claude wrote the eight-document plan accordingly.

Subsequent sessions update specific docs based on monthly reviews and quarterly retrospectives. Material strategy shifts (e.g., adding a non-founder ICP, adding a subscription tier) require a full strategy retrospective, not a tactical edit.

The 1-paragraph plan and the 1-page summary at the top of this README are the canonical short forms. If they ever drift from the long-form plan, the long-form wins and the short form gets updated.
