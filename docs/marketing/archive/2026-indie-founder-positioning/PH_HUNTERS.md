## How to work this list

Read this section before you send anything.

**Rhythm.**
1. Start with 3 HIGH hunters on day 1. Send spaced 30 minutes apart. Don't batch-fire.
2. Wait 48 hours. Most "yes" replies land inside that window. Most ignores stay silent forever.
3. Day 3, send to the next 3 (2 HIGH + 1 MED). Keep going until someone says yes or you've worked the list.
4. Never follow up more than once per hunter, and only if they opened a thread and went quiet. Cold follow-ups on silence = instant no.

**Pipeline rules.**
- Send DMs Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-11am their local time where you can figure it out. Weekends and Monday mornings get buried.
- Personalize every one. The template below has `[HOOK]` brackets for exactly that reason. If you can't find a specific recent hunt for them, skip them or dig harder. A generic DM is worse than no DM.
- Don't offer payment. Every hunter on this list hunts for free as a rule. A payment offer reads as a bribe and gets you blacklisted.
- Don't mention the launch date until they say yes in principle. Lead with the product and the ask. Date comes after.

**Closing the loop.**
- The moment one hunter says yes in writing, message every open thread with: "Hey, wanted to be upfront. Someone else has agreed to hunt. Wanted to thank you for considering and let you know I'd love to stay in touch for a future launch." Don't let anyone hear secondhand that you double-booked.
- Save a note of who said yes, who said no, and who went silent. Those silent ones aren't warm leads for launch #2. They're cold. Treat them that way next time.
- The hunter you land gets early access to the press kit, the demo video, and the gallery captions. Send them 3 days before launch day, not on launch morning.

**The canonical pitch DM (copy, personalize the `[HOOK]` line, send).**

Every pitch below follows this frame. Under 800 characters, ask in sentence 2, hook pulled from their actual recent hunts.

> Hey [name], saw your hunt of [product] last [month]. [Specific one-line reason it stuck with you.] I'm launching Projelli in the next few weeks and wondering if you'd hunt it.
>
> It's a local-first AI workspace for indie founders. Every chat with Claude / GPT / Gemini produces a real Markdown file on your hard drive, with wiki-links and a founder template gallery. BYOK, one-time price ($49 Pro / $99 Lifetime, $29 first 100 buyers), source on GitHub.
>
> Projelli ships persistent memory (RAG over the workspace), an MCP server so Claude Desktop / Cursor / Zed can read your files, side-by-side AI editing, and voice + Ollama for fully-offline work.
>
> 60-sec demo: projelli.com. Happy to send screenshots and the press kit in advance. No pressure if it's not your thing.

That's ~820 characters. Trim the features paragraph if you're over 1000 after personalization.

## If none reply, self-hunt (contingency path)

If you've worked the full list and nobody says yes (or the timing doesn't line up), self-hunt. PH officially supports this now and most successful indie launches in 2025-2026 have been self-hunted.

**How to self-hunt without losing signal:**

1. **Submit under your own PH account** (@jamesondaines or whatever your handle is). Make sure your profile is filled out, meaning bio, photo, links to jamesondaines.com and projelli.com. Empty profiles kill the first-hour momentum because people click through to verify you're real.

2. **Post the maker comment from the same account within 60 seconds of submission.** This is the single most important thing. The maker comment is the thing the PH algorithm weighs heaviest in the first hour, and if the same account submitted, it still counts.

3. **Schedule the launch for Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday at 12:01am PT.** Don't launch Monday (catch-up day, low traffic) or Friday/weekend (dead). Hunted and self-hunted launches have the same timing rules.

4. **Prep 10-15 "engaged comment" friends** from your beta list, LinkedIn, and IndieHackers circle. The loss from not having a hunter is that you don't get the hunter's audience pre-primed. You replace that with your own primed audience: beta testers who comment substantively (not "great launch!") in the first 3 hours.

5. **Do NOT buy hunts or pay for upvotes.** There are companies that sell "guaranteed top-5" hunting services. PH detects them. You will get delisted, permanently. This is the single fastest way to kill the launch.

6. **The maker comment does slightly more work when self-hunting.** It needs to say "I built this because" AND "here's what I'd love help with" in the first 3 paragraphs. People who come to a self-hunted listing are one degree cooler than people who came from a hunter's audience, so the comment has to do the warming-up.

7. **Write to PH support before launch day** at hello@producthunt.com to confirm your submission is fine (Rajiv Ayyangar has a standing public position that self-hunting is welcome). A polite email 48 hours before launch catches any issues. Don't assume.

**Realistic self-hunt outcome vs. hunted outcome:**
- Hunted launch with a HIGH-tier hunter: 200-500 upvotes, top-5 day, 500+ site visitors
- Hunted launch with a MED-tier hunter: 100-250 upvotes, top-10 day, 300+ site visitors
- Good self-hunted launch: 80-200 upvotes, top-10 day, 200+ site visitors
- Mediocre self-hunted launch with weak prep: 20-60 upvotes, no rank, 50 site visitors

The gap between HIGH hunter and good self-hunt is maybe 30-50% on day-1 upvotes. Not nothing, but not the end of the world either. If the full list goes silent after two rounds of DMs, just launch.
