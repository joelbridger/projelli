## Sessions
- **[2026-07-08-wife-onboarding/](2026-07-08-wife-onboarding/)** — first real target-user session.
  An XYPN-network RIA advisor thinks aloud through onboarding. THE foundational research for
  positioning + GTM. Key findings (see its `ANALYSIS.md`):
  - **Two-pillar buying model:** "can we use it?" (compliance) gates FIRST, then "is it useful?"
  - **Compliance is the #1 blocker** — advisors can't put client data in ChatGPT; they're desperate
    for a compliant AI and have none. **Our local-first architecture IS the answer to her #1 pain.**
  - **GTM playbook she handed us:** XYPN approved-list (Jump's path), herd-adoption/firm logos,
    Microsoft-adjacency, court outside compliance consultants (Synergy Compliance), a CCO compliance pack.
  - **Positioning reframe:** lead with "the AI you're actually allowed to use with client data."
  - Onboarding: value prop unclear ("what IS this?"); several fixable UX nits.
