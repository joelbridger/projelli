# Copy Deck — "Start on your own"

> All copy here follows the house voice: first-person where it fits, contractions, concrete nouns, uneven sentence length, **no em dashes**, none of the AI tells (no "leverage / seamless / transform / empower / elevate / unlock"), no "It's not X, it's Y", no italicized sentence-end fragments. See `feedback_marketing_copy_voice.md` + `reference_ai_writing_tells.md`. This audience distrusts marketing language, so accuracy beats polish.
>
> **Hard rule:** the phrase "guaranteed compliant", "fully compliant", or anything implying Keepance makes a user compliant with their firm's rules must never appear. Keepance handles data safety; the firm and the lawyer own policy.

**Heading:** How should Keepance handle your AI?

**Body:** Keepance indexes and searches everything on your computer, with nothing leaving the machine. The one place your text can travel is when an AI writes an answer. Pick how you want that handled. You can change this anytime.

**Option A — Local-only (most private)**
Nothing leaves this computer, ever. Answers are written by a model running on your own machine. You'll need a local model installed, and I'll help you set one up.

**Option B — Cloud (bring your own key)**
Faster and stronger answers from your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google account. Your prompt and the matter text it needs go to that provider. Check your firm's policy before you use this on client work.

**Tertiary action:** Decide later (stays local-only until you choose)

## Security pack (Phase 3) — document copy

**Title:** Keepance security overview for [firm / IT / General Counsel]

**Opening:** This is a plain-English summary of how Keepance handles a lawyer's data, written so your security and ethics reviewers can evaluate it quickly. Every claim here matches how the software actually works. If something isn't finished yet, I say so.

**Section — What Keepance is:** Keepance is a private intelligence layer for a law practice. It runs as a desktop app on the lawyer's own machine. Documents, email, and matters stay in local files the lawyer controls. Keepance answers questions across all of it and shows a citation you can open and check for every answer.

**Section — Where data lives:** (embed the Data Map.) Your files stay on the lawyer's device. Keepance has no content server. We never receive, store, or can read your matter data.

**Section — The three privacy modes and exactly what leaves the machine:**
- **Local-only:** nothing leaves the device. Answers are generated by a model on the machine.
- **Direct (bring your own key):** the lawyer's prompt and the matter text needed for an answer go straight to the AI provider the lawyer chose, using the lawyer's own account. It does not pass through Keepance.
- **Assured (firm option):** AI requests route through your firm's own zero-retention proxy with a managed key and a DPA. The relay stores nothing.

**Section — Keys and accounts:** Keepance never holds AI keys and never charges for AI usage. Solo use needs no account at all. Firm use adds single sign-on, seats, and shared matters.

**Section — Firm collaboration security:** Shared matters sync only as end-to-end encrypted data. The relay can only ever see ciphertext. Ethical walls are enforced by withholding keys, not by hiding things in the interface. Single sign-on is supported through your identity provider.

**Section — Current assurance status:** (State the REAL status from `docs/trust/` + `docs/legal/`. Example shape, fill with truth: "A Data Processing Agreement is available. SOC 2 is [in progress / not yet started] as of [date].") Do not imply more than is true.

**Section — What to ask us:** Questions for your reviewer to send to [contact]. We'll answer in writing.
