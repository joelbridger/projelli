Advisor Prep Hero vs the field

As of June 2026, for press, journalists, and reviewer use · Full press kit

This matrix covers nine AI tools that attorneys, CPAs, consultants, and financial advisors are currently buying or being sold. It shows where Advisor Prep Hero wins, where it loses, and when a prospect should choose the other product. Pricing is in approximate bands; verify with each vendor before publishing.

The short version: every serious competitor in these four professional markets is cloud SaaS. Their privacy story is contractual. Advisor Prep Hero with a local Ollama model is the only option in any of these markets where the absolute claim is architectural rather than contractual: nothing reaches any server other than the machine on the user's desk.

On competitor pricing: all dollar figures are approximate bands, as of 2026. Features and pricing change. Always check the vendor's current page before citing a number. Outbound links are provided below for each incumbent.

Per-incumbent summary cards

Each card states where the incumbent wins honestly, then where Advisor Prep Hero holds the advantage. When you should pick the incumbent instead is always included.

Legal

Clio Duo

Cloud add-on to Clio Manage · approx. $49-59/mo or bundled in Elite

Where Clio Duo wins: It knows your matters, billing codes, client names, and deadlines because it lives inside Clio. Setup is one click for existing Clio subscribers. SOC 2 Type II.

Where Advisor Prep Hero wins: Local zero-egress with Ollama (nothing reaches any server), own your files, much deeper profession templates, and roughly 4-10x cheaper for a solo attorney who already pays for Clio's base tier.

Pick Clio Duo when: your primary pain is AI that knows your matters and billing context, you're already on Clio, and you want a one-click setup with no BYOK friction. Advisor Prep Hero runs beside it, not instead of it.

CoCounsel (Thomson Reuters)

Cloud SaaS · approx. $225-428/mo per seat

Where CoCounsel wins: Westlaw-grounded legal research with Shepard's citation verification. Deep litigation-draft and doc-review capability. Strong brand and bar-association recognition. Zero retention at the LLM layer.

Where Advisor Prep Hero wins: Price (roughly 20x cheaper), zero-egress option via local model, and own-your-files architecture that keeps the actual documents off any vendor's servers.

Pick CoCounsel when: you need citation-verified, Westlaw-grounded research. It is the strongest pure-research tool in the legal market. Advisor Prep Hero does not compete with it on research databases.

Tax

Intuit Assist

Bundled in Lacerte / ProConnect / ProSeries · free with subscription

Where Intuit Assist wins: Already paid for. Pulls 60+ data points directly from the return for planning conversations. Zero incremental cost for existing Intuit subscribers. Frictionless to start.

Where Advisor Prep Hero wins: Local zero-egress with Ollama removes the IRC §7216 third-party disclosure question entirely. Advisor Prep Hero works with Drake (about a third of the small-firm market, which Intuit Assist does not reach). Explicit §7216-consent and WISP templates included.

Pick Intuit Assist when: you're already on Lacerte / ProConnect and want advisory planning AI at no extra cost. Advisor Prep Hero is the companion for the drafting and regulatory documentation that Intuit Assist does not cover, and it is the only option for Drake users.

Blue J ("Ask Blue J")

Cloud SaaS · approx. $1,498/yr (CPA.com promo approx. $998/yr)

Where Blue J wins: Genuine primary-tax-authority research with predictive confidence scoring. Cited, auditable research trail. SOC 2. Research-specific depth no general AI tool matches.

Where Advisor Prep Hero wins: Price (about 10x cheaper), local zero-egress option, and general practice workflows (notices, engagements, WISP templates) that Blue J does not cover.

Pick Blue J when: primary-authority tax research with citations is the job. Advisor Prep Hero does not have a research database and does not try to. Many CPAs use both: Blue J for research, Advisor Prep Hero for drafting and documentation.

Consulting

Microsoft 365 Copilot

Cloud add-on · approx. $18-30/mo per seat

Where M365 Copilot wins: Lives inside Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams. Generates real, formatted PPTX files. Enterprise tenant isolation and no-training policy (enterprise tier). The most frictionless path for anyone already on M365.

Where Advisor Prep Hero wins: A cloud tenant still uploads data to Microsoft's servers. Only a local model truly satisfies a strict NDA no-upload clause. Advisor Prep Hero provides per-client folder isolation as a native primitive, and profession-specific templates that Copilot's general capability does not match.

Pick M365 Copilot when: the deliverable lives in Word or PowerPoint, you have enterprise M365 with Copilot already licensed, and the client's NDA does not require zero-upload. Advisor Prep Hero and Copilot cover different parts of the work.

Gamma

Cloud SaaS · approx. $9-18/mo individual

Where Gamma wins: Polished, designed AI-generated decks in minutes. The best deck-from-prompt output in the market, and at a price most consultants will pay. For final-deliverable deck production it is not closely matched.

Where Advisor Prep Hero wins: Everything that happens before the deck. The research, the client-specific analysis, the draft narrative, the per-client folder of notes. Only a local model honors a strict no-upload clause during the confidential thinking phase. Gamma's free and Plus tiers train by default.

Pick Gamma when: the deliverable is the deck, the client work is done, and you need a fast, polished output. The recommended workflow: think and draft in Advisor Prep Hero (local or BYOK), finish the presentation in Gamma.

Financial advisors

Jump

Cloud SaaS · approx. $75-175/advisor/mo · 35,000+ advisors

Where Jump wins: Purpose-built AI meeting notes with 30+ CRM integrations (Redtail, Salesforce, Wealthbox). SOC 2 Type II. Category leader with the largest installed base in advisor AI. If meeting-notes-to-CRM is the job, Jump is the benchmark.

Where Advisor Prep Hero wins: With a local Ollama model, there is no AI vendor surface for Reg S-P service-provider oversight. Own-your-files: client records are real Word files on the firm's disk, not in a SaaS database. Price (roughly 6-14x cheaper). General drafting, analysis, and documentation that Jump does not cover.

Pick Jump when: the primary pain is meeting notes, follow-ups, and CRM sync, and the practice is prepared for formal vendor approval. Advisor Prep Hero covers the pre- and post-meeting thinking and drafting that Jump does not touch. Many advisors need both.

Zocks

Cloud SaaS · approx. $67-184/mo · SOC 2 Type II

Where Zocks wins: Privacy-forward meeting notes: no audio or video stored, notes only. Native eMoney sync and Smarsh / Global Relay archiving integration. SOC 2 Type II. The privacy-positioned meeting-notes alternative to Jump, with archiving built in.

Where Advisor Prep Hero wins: Even "notes only" means notes left the machine and reached Zocks's cloud. With Advisor Prep Hero on a local model, no note, no draft, no analysis reaches any server. Reg S-P vendor-oversight surface is zero. Own-your-files and general practice drafting beyond meeting notes.

Pick Zocks when: meeting notes with no audio retention and a proven archiving integration are the requirements, and the practice values the privacy-positioned architecture within a cloud model. Advisor Prep Hero covers the drafting and analysis work outside the meeting workflow.

Cross-vertical: the consumer baseline

ChatGPT (consumer: Free / Plus)

Cloud SaaS · $0-20/mo · most widely used AI tool in all four verticals

Where ChatGPT wins: Zero setup. The broadest capability of any consumer AI. Native image generation (DALL-E), voice mode, code interpreter, and the largest GPT Store. Fast for one-off general questions with no sensitive data.

Where Advisor Prep Hero wins: Consumer Free / Plus tiers train on input by default, every conversation goes to OpenAI's servers, and no conversation becomes a file the practitioner owns. The privilege, §7216, Reg S-P, and NDA risks are at their highest with consumer ChatGPT. Advisor Prep Hero is not faster for one-off questions; it is the right tool for confidential client work that needs to live as records the practitioner controls.

Pick ChatGPT when: the question is general-knowledge, the data is not client-sensitive, and you do not need the answer as a persistent file. ChatGPT Business / Enterprise (no-training tiers) are a meaningfully different product from the consumer tiers when confidentiality matters.

The honest summary

Where Advisor Prep Hero wins:
  • The only option in any of these four markets where a local model (Ollama) means nothing reaches any external server. Every other competitor is cloud SaaS; their privacy story is contractual.
  • Own your files in plain Markdown. No vendor lock-in, no disappearing conversation history if the product shuts down or raises prices.
  • Profession-specific workflow templates (legal, tax, consulting, advisor) built into the product.
  • Price: $948/yr Professional versus $1,200-5,000/yr for the purpose-built tools.
  • Works with Drake (the most widely used small-firm tax software) and any other system of record, because it is not trying to integrate with them; it sits beside them.
  • The regulatory forcing functions of 2026 (US v. Heppner for privilege; IRC §7216 / §6713 + FTC Safeguards Rule for tax; SEC Reg S-P deadline June 3 2026 for RIAs; rising NDA no-upload clauses for consulting) all push toward a zero-egress architecture. Advisor Prep Hero is the only commercial product in these markets with that option.
Where incumbents win (do not fight these):
  • Proprietary research databases: CoCounsel / Westlaw for citation-verified case law; Blue J / Checkpoint for primary tax authority. Advisor Prep Hero sits beside these tools, never against them.
  • Deep practice-management integration: Clio Duo (matters, billing, deadlines), Intuit Assist (return-data-aware planning). If the job requires AI that knows what is in the firm's system of record, the bundled tool wins.
  • Meeting-notes and CRM sync: Jump and Zocks are purpose-built for this and have years of head start. Advisor Prep Hero covers the thinking, drafting, and analysis that happens outside the meeting. It isn't built around meeting notes.
  • Polished deck output: Gamma and M365 Copilot-in-PowerPoint produce designed, print-ready decks. Advisor Prep Hero produces structured outlines and basic PPTX. The recommended consulting workflow is: research and draft in Advisor Prep Hero, finish the deck in Gamma or PowerPoint.
  • SOC 2 and signed DPAs: formal vendor approval processes at RIA firms and multi-lawyer firms require these. Advisor Prep Hero is pursuing SOC 2 but does not have it yet. This is a real blocker for some buyers today.
  • Zero-friction setup: bundled tools (Intuit Assist, Clio Duo) and consumer ChatGPT require no API key setup. Advisor Prep Hero requires BYOK. For the most sensitive buyers, leading with the local Ollama path (no API key, no account) removes this friction.

About this matrix

This matrix was built with input from practitioners in each of the four professional verticals, drawing on publicly available vendor information as of June 2026. It is a press and reviewer resource, not a legal, tax, or compliance opinion. Competitive products change; verify any specific claim with the vendor before publishing.

The matrix is honest about gaps because Advisor Prep Hero's positioning depends on practitioners trusting it. If the product was right for every buyer, every comparison would end with "buy Advisor Prep Hero." It is not, and this matrix reflects that.

For the full press kit, fact sheet, screenshots, and downloadable assets, see the Advisor Prep Hero press kit.

Pricing accuracy: all competitor pricing is approximate, as of June 2026, and sourced from vendor pages and third-party aggregators. Pricing in SaaS changes frequently. Always verify with the vendor's current pricing page before citing a specific dollar figure.

Local-model accuracy: "nothing leaves your machine" claims apply specifically to users running a local AI model (e.g., Ollama). When using a cloud provider via BYOK (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google), the prompt is sent to that provider's servers under the user's own API key and terms of service. Advisor Prep Hero never receives that data; the provider does.
