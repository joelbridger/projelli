Who it's for

Built for professionals who take confidentiality seriously.

Keepance isn't a general productivity tool. It's built specifically for work that can't leave the room.

Solo & small-firm attorneys

Case files, deposition notes, discovery documents, privilege logs, client intake summaries. All of it stays on your machine. Keepance can find contradictions across depositions, draft privilege log entries, and build case timelines — without any of it touching a cloud server.

ABA Formal Opinion 512 (2024) addresses attorney use of AI tools and is favorable to local-first approaches. Ask your bar counsel.

Tax preparers & CPAs

Your clients' SSNs, returns, and financial statements never leave your machine. Keepance handles engagement letter drafting, return pre-review, research memos, and audit defense prep — all offline, all yours.

IRC §7216 restricts unauthorized disclosure of tax return information. Running AI locally is the clearest way to stay on the right side of it. Confirm with your tax counsel.

Independent consultants & agencies

Your NDAs say client data stays confidential. Your workflow depends on AI. Keepance is where those two facts coexist. Synthesize client discovery, draft confidential memos, and prepare presentations — without uploading a single line of client context to someone else's server.

Many client contracts now explicitly prohibit uploading work product to AI services. Keepance sidesteps the clause entirely.

A note on compliance claims

The ABA and IRC §7216 references on this page are informational, not legal advice. Keepance's local-first architecture is designed to reduce your compliance surface — but it doesn't eliminate your professional obligations. Verify how Keepance fits your specific practice with your bar counsel or tax advisor.

What "local-first" actually means

When you use Keepance, your documents and API key stay on your machine. Your prompts go directly to Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google — Keepance never sees them. The AI provider you choose does see your prompts, which is governed by their terms. If that's also a concern, Keepance supports local models via Ollama (fully offline).
