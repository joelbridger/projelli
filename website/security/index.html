Security and data handling.

Updated 2026-07-10 · Forwardable to a CCO, IT reviewer, or malpractice carrier

The short version. I built Advisor Prep Hero so private client work lives in a folder you control. Your AI key stays in your computer's keychain. In Direct mode, AI requests go straight to your provider. In Local-only mode, no AI prompt or file goes to a cloud AI. Firm sharing is different: if you choose to share a client, your client data is encrypted on your device; the relay stores only ciphertext and opaque handles and never sees client names or documents. This page separates those paths plainly.

This page is for advisors and the IT or compliance people who review tools for them. It covers where data lives, who can read it, how keys work, what the audit trail records, and where a Firm plan changes the picture. If a question is not answered here, email security@keepance.com.

What I do with your data

Private work stays on your machine

By default, your workspace is a folder you choose on your own computer. Your private files live there, and your imported email and search index stay on your computer. Word .docx is the main document format. Markdown is still available for utility text files, but it is not the whole product.

API keys in your OS keychain

When you configure Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google for Direct mode, I store your key in your computer's secure keychain: Keychain on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows, and the Secret Service on Linux. Your Direct-mode key is not sent to my servers.

Direct AI requests go to your provider

In Direct mode, a request to Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google travels from your computer straight to that provider with your key. I am not in the middle of that request. The provider receives the prompt and any file content you choose to include.

Local AI keeps AI work on your computer

With the built-in local model or Ollama in Local-only mode, the AI runs on your computer. No AI prompt or file is sent to a cloud AI. That setting does not turn off a connector or Firm sharing that you have separately chosen to use.

Imported email is encrypted on your device

If you import email, it is kept in an encrypted local database on your computer and searched there. The same confidentiality choice you make for AI requests applies when imported email is used as context.

Local audit trail

Advisor Prep Hero records AI actions in an append-only audit log on your computer. The log is encrypted at rest, shows the action's destination and relevant source context, and is there for you or your reviewer to inspect.

Firm sharing is optional and encrypted

Firm members can choose to share a client. Only the shared notes and documents they co-edit are sent through the relay. Each shared client has its own encryption key, held by authorized members' computers. Your client data is encrypted on your device; the relay stores only ciphertext and opaque handles and never sees client names or documents. Your other files, email, and search index stay on your computer.

Analytics are opt-in

The desktop app does not send analytics unless you turn them on in Settings. If you do, it sends a random install ID, app version, platform, and lifecycle events. It does not send file content, prompts, or email. This marketing site uses Plausible Analytics.

What the data flow actually looks like

Your choices matter. These are the main paths in the desktop app:

Path A: Direct cloud AI with your key

  1. You choose a cloud AI provider and type a prompt in Advisor Prep Hero.
  2. Advisor Prep Hero reads your key from your computer's keychain.
  3. Your computer sends the prompt and selected context directly to that provider's API, under your account.
  4. The provider returns a response directly to your machine.
  5. Advisor Prep Hero saves the result in your local workspace.

Who can read the request: you and the AI provider you chose. Their handling of it is governed by your agreement and settings with that provider. I do not make that choice for you.

Path B: Local AI on your computer

  1. You select Local-only mode and type a prompt.
  2. Advisor Prep Hero sends the prompt to the local model on your computer.
  3. The model generates a response on your hardware.
  4. Advisor Prep Hero saves the result in your local workspace.

Who can read that AI request: you. No AI prompt or file is sent to a cloud AI. A separately configured connector or shared Firm client can still make its own network connection, so turn those off too if your rule is that no client content may leave the computer.

Path C: Optional Firm sharing

  1. A Firm member explicitly shares a client with the firm.
  2. Shared notes and documents being co-edited are encrypted on the member's computer.
  3. The encrypted update is sent to the relay so authorized colleagues can receive it.
  4. Each authorized colleague decrypts it on their own computer.

Who can read the shared content: the people authorized for that client. Your client data is encrypted on your device; the relay stores only ciphertext and opaque handles and never sees client names or documents. A firm can also use single sign-on with Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, or another OIDC-compatible identity provider.

What my services do handle

Advisor Prep Hero is local-first, not server-free. Depending on what you turn on, my services handle these narrow jobs:

  • License activation and update checks. The app contacts the relevant service to confirm a license or look for a new version. These requests do not include your workspace content.
  • Firm sign-in and administration. Firm accounts, seats, invitations, and single sign-on settings need a service so the right people can join the right firm.
  • Firm collaboration. When a client is shared, client data is encrypted on the member's device; the relay stores only ciphertext and opaque handles and never sees client names or documents. It is not a readable server-side workspace.
  • The browser demo. The public demo can send messages through a shared relay when a visitor has not added their own key. Do not put client data in the browser demo. The desktop app's Direct mode does not use that relay.
  • Optional analytics and support. Analytics are off until you enable them. A message you deliberately send through a support or bug-report form goes to support.

External connectors are separate again. When you connect Outlook, Gmail, OneDrive, a CRM, or another source, the app talks to that source from your computer and imports the data locally. That connection follows the permissions and privacy terms of the service you connected.

Key storage

Advisor Prep Hero uses your operating system's keychain to store your own cloud AI keys:

  • macOS: Keychain.
  • Windows: Windows Credential Manager.
  • Linux: the Secret Service.

I don't put those keys in a plain-text app configuration file or send your own cloud key to my servers. The browser demo and the Firm-only managed-key path are different services, and are called out before you use them.

What I don't have yet

Current limits, stated plainly

  • No unqualified security promises. I will not tell you that a local-first design makes every risk disappear. Your device, your chosen AI provider, your connectors, and your firm's own controls still matter.
  • Firm sharing is not the same as a private workspace. It is an opt-in collaboration feature. Client data is encrypted on the member's device; the relay stores only ciphertext and opaque handles and never sees client names or documents while authorized colleagues work together.
  • A DPA needs real review. A template is available on request, but it must be reviewed by qualified counsel before anyone signs it.
  • Cloud AI has its own rules. Direct mode sends content to the provider you choose. Review that provider's agreement and settings before you use it with client data.

I would rather give you the real tradeoff than a polished answer. Keep private work local if it belongs only on your computer. Use Firm sharing only when the right colleagues need access. Use a local model when no AI prompt or file should reach a cloud AI.

Frequently asked questions

Can Advisor Prep Hero read my documents?

I do not receive a readable copy of your private workspace. The app reads files locally to show and search them. If you choose Direct cloud AI, your selected content goes to that provider. If you choose Firm sharing, authorized colleagues can read the shared material after their computers decrypt it. Your client data is encrypted on your device; the relay stores only ciphertext and opaque handles and never sees client names or documents.

What does my cloud AI provider receive?

In Direct mode, the provider receives your prompt and the file content you chose to include. Its retention, training, and account controls come from your agreement and settings with that provider. I do not change those terms, so review them before you use cloud AI with client data.

What if I want literally nothing to leave my machine?

Use Local-only mode with the built-in local model or Ollama. That keeps AI prompts and files off cloud AI. Also do not use Firm sharing or an external connector for that client, because those are separate connections you control.

Is there a server-side copy of my workspace?

Your private workspace is not copied to a readable Advisor Prep Hero server. For Firm sharing, your client data is encrypted on your device; the relay stores only ciphertext and opaque handles and never sees client names or documents, so colleagues can collaborate. It is not a general cloud backup for the rest of your workspace. Keep your own backup routine for the files on your computer.

What is in the audit log?

The local audit log records AI actions and the information needed to understand where the action went and what source context was used. It is append-only and encrypted at rest on your computer. The app gives you the record so you can inspect it with a compliance reviewer.

Do you have a Data Processing Agreement?

There is a template available on request. It needs review by qualified counsel before execution, especially when a firm plans to use shared collaboration or a managed AI route. Email legal@keepance.com for it.

What happens to my data if Advisor Prep Hero shuts down?

Your private files remain in the folder you chose. A Word .docx file remains a Word file and can be opened outside Advisor Prep Hero. Text files remain ordinary files too. That answer is about private local work, not a promise about a separate Firm collaboration service.

Do you support multi-user or enterprise deployments?

Yes, on the Firm plan. It includes an admin console, seats, client-by-client sharing, and single sign-on through Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, or another OIDC-compatible identity provider. Your client data is encrypted on your device; the relay stores only ciphertext and opaque handles and never sees client names or documents.
