Windows security prompt: what to expect

Windows shows a "Windows protected your PC" warning for any newly released app that hasn't been downloaded by enough people yet. It's a reputation system, not a virus flag. Keepance is signed by the real developer (publisher: Jameson Daines). You can verify this in the dialog itself.
