How it works

Direct connection

When you run a workflow, the request goes from your machine to your AI provider. Keepance is never in the middle. Your files stay local; only the text you choose to send goes to the provider you picked, under the terms you agreed to with them.

2

Files on disk

Every analysis saves as a plain Markdown file in a folder you control. Auditable, searchable, portable. Standard format that survives any software change, and one you can produce at any time.

Your keys, your costs

Bring your own Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini key. You control the API spend and the provider relationship. Keepance charges once at purchase, never again, and never touches your keys.

Jump and Zocks are purpose-built for meeting notes and CRM sync, and that's not what Keepance does. If automating post-meeting admin is your main need, buy one of them. Keepance is for the private drafting and analysis of your most sensitive client work, and for the firm whose compliance officer doesn't want any AI vendor receiving client data at all.
