For Financial Advisors

For advisors who won't put client data in someone else's cloud.

Many won't run that through a vendor's cloud AI, and that's completely reasonable. Keepance never sees your work. Your client data stays out of a vendor's hands
Cloud AI tools that receive client information introduce a third party into your data path. With Keepance, there is no Keepance server in the middle. The AI request goes from your machine to the provider you chose, under the terms you agreed to with them.
No vendor in your data path
Your client data does not pass through any server of ours. There is no Keepance vendor relationship sitting between you and your work. The fewer places your client data can travel, the fewer things there are to worry about.
Work product that is yours to keep
Every AI chat becomes a real Markdown file in a folder you control. Your work product stays on your machine, searchable and portable, ready to produce whenever you need it. Keeping your work on your own machine is one less place your client data lives. It does not replace your policies, your retention obligations, or your judgment.

How it works

Your files stay on your machine. Keepance is never in the path.

Want to see the exact data path? Read the full breakdown.

1

Direct connection

When you run a workflow, the request goes from your machine to your AI provider. Keepance is never in the middle. Your files stay local; only the text you choose to send goes to the provider you picked, under the terms you agreed to with them.

2

Files on disk

Every analysis saves as a plain Markdown file in a folder you control. Auditable, searchable, portable. Standard format that survives any software change, and one you can produce at any time.

3

Your keys, your costs

Bring your own Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini key. You control the API spend and the provider relationship. Keepance charges once at purchase, never again, and never touches your keys.
