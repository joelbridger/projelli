# Acme Budget, Content Strategy

## The thesis

Acme Budget wins SEO in 2026 by being the most honest and specific voice on freelance personal finance. Not the prettiest, not the most polished. The one that names the exact dollar amount your Q3 estimate should be and shows the exact spreadsheet formula to back it up.

## Channels in priority order

1. **SEO (60% of effort).** Long-form guides on acmebudget.com/guides/, each paired with a free in-browser tool.
2. **Twitter and LinkedIn (25%).** Maya posts as herself, 3x/week on Twitter, 2x/week on LinkedIn.
3. **Newsletter (15%).** Weekly Sunday-night email to the paid user list, with a public archive.

No YouTube in year one. No TikTok. No podcast.

## The SEO content calendar

Fifteen guides over the first 6 months, published every other Monday. Each one is a real tool plus a long answer. Target keyword difficulty under 30 per [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/).

Modeled on what [Keeper](https://www.keepertax.com/) and [Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com/) have done successfully for 3+ years.

## Twitter pattern

Three shapes, one per week:

1. **The specific-number tweet.** "Freelance writer I interviewed yesterday. Gross income last year: $87,400. Taxes actually owed: $18,200. Taxes set aside: $4,100. She put $14,100 on a credit card. This is the median freelancer experience."
2. **The teardown.** A quoted tweet of a bad personal-finance take, responded to with the specific reason it doesn't work for someone with lumpy income.
3. **The tiny tool.** A Tweetdeck-sized screenshot of a calculator from acmebudget.com/guides/ with one paragraph of context.

## LinkedIn pattern

Two shapes, one per week:

1. **The weekly retro.** "What I learned building Acme Budget this week" in 8 sentences, one image.
2. **The freelancer story.** An anonymized story from a user interview with permission, 10 sentences, no CTA.

LinkedIn is the long-form surface for the 1-3 year freelancer ICP specifically. Twitter is the general-audience surface that occasionally converts.

## Newsletter

Weekly on Sunday at 7 PM PT. Three sections every week:

1. **The Sunday number.** One real freelancer stat with a source link.
2. **The tool.** One small feature Acme Budget shipped this week.
3. **The open question.** One question I'm wrestling with, with a reply-to-me CTA.

Published via [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/) (single-operator simple, $9/mo). Public archive on acmebudget.com/newsletter/.

## What I'm not doing

- No generic SEO content ("What is a budget?") because the target ICP already knows.
- No paid ads driving to guides until a guide is ranking organically first.
- No content partnerships with finance influencers. The audience sees through those.
- No AI-written content. Every guide is drafted by me and edited for accuracy.
