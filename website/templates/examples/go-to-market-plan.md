# Acme Budget, Go-to-Market Plan

## The 6-month distribution bet

Three channels, in priority order. I'll run all three but kill any that doesn't show signal by month 3.

### Channel 1: SEO on high-intent freelance tax queries

Freelancers Google the same 30 questions every year around Q1 and Q4. "How much should I set aside for taxes as a freelancer" has an estimated 12,100 monthly US searches according to [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/). I'm targeting 40 of these queries with long-form answers on acmebudget.com/guides/.

Each guide is a real tool (tax-rate calculator, quarterly estimator, runway calculator) plus a 2,500-word answer. The tool is the link bait. [Keeper](https://www.keepertax.com/), [Found](https://found.com/), and [Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com/) have ranked this way for 3+ years.

Target by month 6: 15 guides published, 2-3 ranking on page 1, 8,000 organic visits/month, 400 trial signups.

### Channel 2: Twitter and LinkedIn, niche freelance communities

I'll post 3x/week on Twitter and 2x/week on LinkedIn, aimed at the specific population of 1-3 year freelancers. Content pattern from my audit of [@SahilBloom](https://twitter.com/SahilBloom), [@level](https://twitter.com/level), and the [Freelance Writing Jobs Slack](https://slack.com/): mini-stories, real numbers, no buzzwords.

No paid promotion. Growth through genuine community participation in subreddits r/freelance and r/digitalnomad, the [Freelancer's Union forum](https://www.freelancersunion.org/), and three private Slack communities (Superpath, Peak Freelance, Contra).

Target by month 6: 3,500 followers, one tweet/thread per month crossing 100K impressions, 200 trial signups from social.

### Channel 3: Accountant partnerships

Freelancers trust their accountant more than any influencer. I'm reaching out to 30 solo/small CPA firms that specialize in Schedule C filers, offering a free year of Acme Budget for their clients in exchange for referral links.

Reference: [Bench](https://www.bench.co/)'s early growth leaned hard on CPA referrals. [Collective](https://www.collective.com/) does it today.

Target by month 6: 5 active CPA partners, 80 trial signups from their lists.

## Launch channels, in order

1. **Beta waitlist** (weeks 1-4): collect 400 emails via the Twitter bio, a simple landing page on acmebudget.com, and a post on [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/).
2. **Private beta** (weeks 5-8): invite the first 40 to try it, run 8 founder-led user interviews each week.
3. **Product Hunt** (week 12): launch as a paid v1.0, targeting #1 Product of the Day. Requires 400 hunters lined up pre-launch.
4. **Show HN** (week 13): a technical post about the tax-bucket algorithm and Plaid retry logic.
5. **SEO compound** (months 4-12): the long tail starts paying in month 4.
