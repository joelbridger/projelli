# Acme Budget, Primary Persona

## Persona: The Second-Year Solo Freelancer

**Name (for the team to reference):** Alex Reyes

**Age:** 32

**Location:** Austin, TX

**Role:** Freelance product designer, 2 years solo after 5 years at two startups

## What Alex does on a Tuesday

Wakes up at 7:15. Checks Slack for two active clients. Opens a Figma file, works on a feature spec until 11. Fields a rush request from a third client. Thinks about whether they should take it. Remembers rent is due next week. Takes the work. Closes the laptop at 6:30 feeling productive but unclear on whether this month is profitable.

## Income shape

- 2-4 active clients at any time
- Monthly income ranges from $6,200 (bad month) to $18,400 (great month)
- Average trailing 12 months: $11,800/month
- Paid via [Stripe](https://stripe.com/), [Wise](https://wise.com/), direct deposit, and occasionally check
- Invoicing via [Harvest](https://www.getharvest.com/)
- Bank: [Chase](https://www.chase.com/) business checking + personal checking

## What Alex currently uses

- A [Notion](https://www.notion.so/) page for a running budget, updated every few weeks
- A Google Sheet their accountant sent them for Schedule C prep
- [Mint](https://www.mint.com/) (deprecating) for transaction categorization, which they ignore
- Post-its on their monitor for quarterly tax estimates

## The three biggest problems

1. **Surprise tax bills.** Q4 2024 Alex owed $9,400 and had $4,100 in the account. They put the rest on a credit card and carried the balance for 6 months. Never wants that to happen again.
2. **"Am I actually making enough?"** Alex doesn't know their real effective hourly rate across all clients. They suspect one client is dragging down the average but haven't had time to figure out which.
3. **Runway anxiety.** When a client ghosts, Alex doesn't know how long their savings will last. They check their bank balance 4-6 times a day.

## What Alex tried before

Alex tried [YNAB](https://www.ynab.com/) for 3 weeks in early 2025. Bounced because the zero-based method kept breaking every time a late invoice hit. Tried [Copilot](https://copilot.money/) for a month. Loved the design, but there was no "set aside for taxes" feature and they dropped it.

## Quote from the interview

> "I don't need it to be pretty. I need it to tell me, on Sunday night, whether I'm okay. And I need it to have already moved the tax money out of the main account before I see it."

## What would make Alex tell their friends

If Acme Budget, in its first 30 days of use, successfully held back a tax reserve big enough to cover the Q1 estimated payment with no action from Alex, they will tell every freelancer in their Slack community. That's the moment of trust that flips them from skeptical trial to active evangelist.

## What would make Alex churn

A Plaid outage that misses a week of transactions, followed by an incorrect tax-reserve number. One bad data event in the first 60 days.
