# Acme Budget, User Interview Plan

## Who I'm trying to talk to

Ten US freelancers, all 1-3 years into solo work, all earning between $60K and $180K annually, all with at least two concurrent clients. I want five designers / writers and five developers / consultants. The goal is not a statistically valid sample. The goal is ten specific stories about the last surprise tax bill.

## Screener questions

Before I book a 30-minute call, the person answers five questions on a Typeform:

1. How long have you been a full-time freelancer?
2. How many concurrent clients do you have right now?
3. What's your best guess at your 2025 gross income, in a range?
4. When was the last time you were surprised by a tax bill, and by how much?
5. What tool, if any, are you using to track your finances today?

Answers 1-3 filter for ICP fit. Answer 4 filters for the specific pain I'm solving. Answer 5 tells me what I'm replacing.

## The script

All interviews are 30 minutes over Zoom. Recorded with consent via [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai/). I open with the same paragraph every time:

> "Thanks for making time. I'm not selling you anything today. I'm building a personal finance tool for freelancers and I'm trying to learn whether the problem I think exists actually exists for people like you. If I ask something that feels weird or invasive, just say 'pass' and we'll move on."

Then eight questions, roughly in this order:

1. Walk me through how you got paid last month. First invoice to money-in-account.
2. When you see that money hit your account, what do you do with it in the first week?
3. Do you have a system for setting aside money for taxes? Tell me about it.
4. When was the last time you were surprised by a bill or expense?
5. How often do you check your bank balance?
6. What happens when a client pays late?
7. If you think about the next 12 months, what's your single biggest financial fear?
8. If you had a magic wand for freelance finances, what would you make it do?

Question 5 is the killer. Every interview I've done so far has had the same answer: "Five or six times a day." That's the hook.

## Outreach

Four channels:

1. **My own network:** 40 cold DMs to freelancers I've loosely known for 2+ years.
2. **Public Twitter post** with a Typeform link and a $25 Amazon gift card incentive.
3. **r/freelance** post following their AMA-style research rules.
4. **[Respondent.io](https://www.respondent.io/)** as a backup if I can't fill the slots organically.

## What I'm watching for

Three themes from the first 4 interviews I've already run:

1. Everyone has a "tax account" that gets raided. Every person.
2. Nobody can name their effective hourly rate across all clients.
3. Everyone is checking their balance 4-6 times per day and cannot stop.

If the next 6 interviews corroborate these three themes, I have product-market-conviction-level evidence. If I start hearing a fourth theme I didn't expect, the product scope gets reconsidered.

## After the call

Within 24 hours, I write a one-page memo per interview: top three quotes, the biggest surprise, the contradiction with my hypothesis, the concrete feature implication. All ten go into one synthesis document in Keepance via the [multi-interview synthesis](/blog/how-i-built-keepance-in-8-weeks) workflow.

## Incentive budget

$25 gift card per completed 30-minute call. Ten calls = $250. Cheap for the cleanest founder-user signal available.
