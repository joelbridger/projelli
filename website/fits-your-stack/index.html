Honest about scope

What Keepance is not

Being clear about what Keepance does not do is as important as what it does. If you need one of these, use the tool built for it. Keepance reads from them and answers across them.

Not a CRM

It does not manage contacts, tasks, or your pipeline, and it does not replace Wealthbox, Redtail, or Salesforce. Keepance reads from your CRM and answers across everything around it.

Not a financial planner

It does not build the plan or give advice, and it does not replace RightCapital, eMoney, or MoneyGuidePro. It preps you before the review and drafts the follow-up from the real file.

Not a custodian

It never holds accounts or moves money. Schwab, Fidelity, and Pershing keep doing that. Keepance just reads the statements and answers questions about them.

Not a meeting note-taker

It does not record or transcribe your meetings, and it does not replace Jump or Zocks. Keepance owns the client file outside the meeting: years of documents and email, answered with citations.

Keepance does one thing: it is the private place all of a client's context comes together and answers you back, with proof, without routing your client data through a server you do not control.
