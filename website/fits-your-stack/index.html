Where Advisor Prep Hero fits in your existing stack

Each vertical has a system of record it already runs on. Advisor Prep Hero is not that system. It is the workspace where the AI work happens, and it hands results back to the tools you already use.

Attorneys

You already have: Clio, Filevine, or similar

You run your matter management, billing, and docketing in Clio, Filevine, or whatever system your firm uses. That stays exactly where it is. Advisor Prep Hero handles the AI work: the research memo, the deposition summary, the privilege log draft, the contradictions analysis. You bring a PDF or document in from the matter folder, work with it privately, and export the result as Word or PDF to file from there.

Round trip: import a deposition PDF from your matter folder, ask Advisor Prep Hero to find contradictions and flag the page cites, export the analysis as Word or PDF, attach it to the matter in Clio. Nothing you worked on ever left your machine.

Honest about scope

What Advisor Prep Hero is not

Being clear about what Advisor Prep Hero does not do is as important as what it does. If you need one of these things, use the tool built for it.

Not a practice management system

Advisor Prep Hero does not track matters, dockets, deadlines, or billing. It does not replace Clio, Filevine, or MyCase. Use those for case management.

Not a CRM

Advisor Prep Hero does not manage contacts, pipelines, or client relationships. It does not replace Salesforce, Redtail, or HubSpot. Use your CRM for those.

Not a document storage system

Advisor Prep Hero is a workspace for active AI work, not a permanent archive or a DMS. Store your finalized files wherever you store everything else: NetDocuments, SharePoint, iManage, your own folder system.

Not a billing platform

Advisor Prep Hero does not track time, generate invoices, or process payments. It does not replace Clio Payments, QuickBooks, or your firm billing system.

Advisor Prep Hero does one thing: gives you a private, local-first workspace where AI work can happen on your most sensitive files, without routing them through a server you do not control. That is a narrow job, and it is the right job for what it is.
