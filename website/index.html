Who it's for

Built for professionals who take confidentiality seriously.

Keepance isn't a general productivity tool. It's built for work that can't leave the room.

Solo & small-firm attorneys

Case files, deposition notes, discovery documents, privilege logs, client intake summaries, all on your machine. Keepance finds contradictions across depositions, drafts privilege log entries, and builds case timelines without touching a cloud server.

ABA Formal Opinion 512 (2024) is favorable to local-first AI approaches. A February 2026 S.D.N.Y. ruling (United States v. Heppner) found that consumer AI use without attorney direction offers no privilege protection. Counsel-directed, local-first workflows address that risk directly. For patent work, the EU's absolute-novelty rule means cloud AI use before filing carries patentability implications worth discussing with IP counsel. Verify all of this with your bar counsel.

Tax preparers & CPAs

Your clients' SSNs, returns, and financial statements never leave your machine. Keepance handles engagement letter drafting, return pre-review, research memos, and audit defense prep, all offline, all yours.

IRC §6713 imposes strict-liability civil penalties of $250 per unauthorized disclosure of tax return information, with no intent required. IRC §7216 makes knowing or reckless disclosure a federal criminal misdemeanor. The FTC Safeguards Rule (16 CFR Part 314) requires a Written Information Security Program for every covered practitioner, with no small-practitioner exemption. Local-first AI simplifies all three. Confirm your specific situation with a tax attorney.
