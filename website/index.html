The Client Map

Every client has a story. Keepance keeps it usable.

A Client Map is a living profile built from the materials you already have: files, emails, notes, intake forms, meeting summaries, and guided interviews. It captures what matters, what changed, what is missing, and where each detail came from.

01

People & relationships

Know the family members, companies, decision-makers, beneficiaries, opposing parties, advisors, and other people who shape the work.

02

History & timeline

See what happened, when it happened, who said it, and which document or email proves it.

03

Goals & preferences

Remember what the client cares about, how they like to communicate, what they worry about, and what good service means to them.

04

Risks & open questions

See contradictions, missing details, incomplete intake, unsigned documents, stale information, and issues worth reviewing.

05

Firm context

Apply your firm's philosophy, preferred language, templates, tone, and review standards across client work.

The result is shared client memory your team can actually use.

Keepance can read across a client's file and flag the things worth checking: inconsistent dates, missing signatures, stale information, open promises, contradictory statements, and facts that appear in one place but not another. It proposes. You decide. Every flag points back to a source.

Where your data goes

Privacy you can explain.

Keepance shows where your data lives, where each AI request goes, and which mode you are using before you run it. Your files live in folders you control, and every AI action shows where the request is going before it runs.

Local model

Run with Ollama for work that should stay entirely on your machine. The request never leaves your computer.

Bring your own AI key

Use Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google directly from your own account. The provider sees the prompt. Keepance does not route it through our server.

Firm deployment

For teams that need admin controls, shared client files, ethical walls, and a more formal review path.

No hidden data path. Every action shows a clear egress indicator: your machine only, your chosen AI provider, or your firm's Assured route when enabled. You can see what is being sent, where it is going, and which client or matter it belongs to. Keepance does not keep a copy of your client files in a Keepance cloud.
