FEATURES

A workspace for the messy projects

From the first vague idea to the version you actually launch, Projelli keeps every piece of a project in one place you can come back to.

AI chat that turns into real files

The chat is not a throwaway window. Every conversation leaves behind documents you can edit, rename, and come back to, the same way you would with any other file on your computer.

Lives on your machine

Your files stay on your hard drive. No account, no sync, no cloud. Works on a plane, in a cabin, on a couch with the Wi-Fi unplugged.

Markdown editor

A proper Markdown editor with wiki-style links, automatic backlinks, split panes, and a live preview. Build out a second brain if you want one.

Version history

Every edit is saved. Undo, redo, and a diff view so you can experiment without worrying about losing the version you actually liked.

Pick your own model

Bring your own API keys for Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini. Switch between them per conversation. If one of them gets worse, you are not stuck with it.

Research & citations

Projelli keeps a running list of the sources and citations behind every document, so when someone asks you where a number came from, you can actually answer.

Most AI chats evaporate the moment you close the tab. In Projelli, every conversation drops real documents into your project folder: plans, research notes, character sheets, strategy guides. Edit them inline, keep old versions, link them to the rest of your notes.

  • Documents get saved to your folder, not buried in chat history
  • You can see what the AI did and walk it back
  • Follow-up prompts rewrite the document in place
  • Export to Markdown or PDF whenever you want

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to get a project out of your head

No setup, no tutorial. Open the app, pick a template, start typing.

1

Pick a template

Start from one of the 15 founder templates or a blank project. Each template already knows the documents you probably need and helps you fill them in.

2

Talk to the AI

Describe the project in your own words. The AI asks a few follow-up questions, then drafts the documents you would have written yourself if you had two extra hours.

3

Edit and link

Fix up the drafts in the Markdown editor, link related notes with wiki-links, and keep iterating until the whole project sits in one place you trust.
