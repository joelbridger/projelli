AI WORKSPACE

Chat that leaves something behind

Most AI chats evaporate the moment you close the tab. In Projelli, every conversation drops real Markdown files into your workspace folder: plans, research notes, customer interviews, draft pitches. Edit inline, keep old versions, link them to anything else.

  • Conversations save to your folder, not buried in chat history
  • Every AI action is logged and reversible
  • Follow-up prompts rewrite the document in place
  • Export to Markdown, PDF, Word, or PowerPoint
CONNECTED KNOWLEDGE

A workspace that keeps your thinking connected

Any document in Projelli can link to any other. Wiki-style links, automatic backlinks, and full-text search across the whole workspace. Split panes let you keep one file open while you write in another.

  • [[Wiki-links]] between any two documents
  • Automatic backlinks so nothing gets orphaned
  • Split-pane editing
  • Full-text search across your entire workspace
LOCAL-FIRST

Your data lives on your computer

Projelli stores everything locally using plain Markdown files on disk. No account, nothing syncing to someone else's server. Open the workspace folder in Finder and your conversations are right there as files you can read in any text editor.

  • Plain Markdown files in a folder you pick
  • Works fully offline (except for AI calls)
  • No account required, no telemetry
  • Native desktop app built on Tauri (Rust)
