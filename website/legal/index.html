Deposition Contradiction Finder

Analyzes deposition transcript excerpts for internal inconsistencies and contradictions with prior testimony, flagged with line and page references and organized by topic. Produces suggested follow-up questions.

Evidence Gap Analyzer

Given your legal theory and the evidence currently in hand, identifies what is missing, what would strengthen the case, and where to look. Produces a gap analysis organized by evidence category with discovery priorities.

Privilege Log Drafter

Drafts a properly formatted privilege log with attorney-client and work product assertions for each entry. Produces a table formatted to common court standards with a cover note and attorney review flags.

Case Timeline Builder

Builds a chronological case timeline from events you provide, organized into phases (pre-incident, incident, post-incident, litigation) with statute of limitations flags and procedural deadline notes.

Client Intake Synthesizer

Turns intake call notes into three structured documents in one file: a Matter Summary, a Conflict Check Memo with parties to search, and a Preliminary Scope of Work with suggested next steps.

Discovery Document Triage

Given a production set description or document index, builds a prioritized review plan: what to read immediately, what to review before depositions, what to spot-check, and targeted search terms organized by issue.

Patent Disclosure Draft

Structures an invention disclosure from an inventor's description for use by patent counsel in drafting the application. Covers technical field, background, summary, detailed description, a claims sketch (starting point only), abstract, and a pre-filing checklist. Includes a local-first data notice. Pair with Ollama for zero-network-exposure drafting.

Contract Review Checklist

Generates a contract-type-specific review checklist from the matter details you provide, covering key risk areas, flagged provisions, and suggested negotiation points organized by priority. You supply the contract; Advisor Prep Hero structures your review notes. Verify all legal positions before advising a client.

Transactional Matter Summary

Builds a deal summary from transaction details you enter: parties, structure, key terms, open issues, and conditions to close. Formats output as a working memo for the matter file, covering the items most relevant to your transaction type.

Estate Planning Client Summary

Drafts a planning summary from client intake data: goals, existing documents, estate composition, and applicable planning tools. Organizes output by planning category so you can review the full picture before the follow-up meeting.
