The core loop

Keepance works in three steps: you ask a question, the AI responds, and a real Markdown file appears on your hard drive.

Step 1

You ask

Type your question into the chat panel. Paste documents, images, PDFs. The AI has full context of your workspace.

Step 2

AI responds

Watch the response stream in real-time. Stop at any point, edit the message, rerun with different parameters.

Step 3

File appears

Save the conversation as a permanent Markdown file in your workspace. Edit, link to other files, version-track changes.

The BYOK difference

Your API key goes directly to Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google. It never passes through Keepance's servers. Your files stay on your machine. Keepance never sees your work, never stores it, never logs it. When you send a prompt to a cloud model, it goes straight from your computer to that provider under your own account; Keepance is not in the path. With Ollama, nothing leaves your machine at all. You control the API call. You control the cost. And if you ever stop using Keepance, your work doesn't disappear: it's already on your hard drive as plain Markdown files.
