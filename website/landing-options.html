The named concepts

The four ideas the brand is built around. These appear as real sections on the page.

Client Map

A living profile of every client, matter, or household: the people, history, goals, risks, open questions, preferences, and your firm's way of serving them.

Context Completeness

See what you know, what you assume, and what you still need to ask. A risk and quality check, not just a productivity feature.

Guided Client Interview

Keepance walks you through the questions that turn a folder of documents into a full client picture.

Firm Philosophy

Keepance stores your firm's way of working and applies it as context across every map, memo, draft, and handoff.

Why it matters

Before and after

Before Keepance

Client knowledge is scattered, fragile, and trapped in memory. New staff need hand-holding. Old context gets missed. Important facts hide in email. Client nuance disappears between meetings.

After Keepance

Each client has a living map. The firm can ask questions, verify sources, see what's missing, prepare for meetings, onboard staff, create notes, and serve with continuity.
