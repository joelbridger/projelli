## Household Profile

Robert Hendricks (64) is a retired civil engineer. Susan Hendricks (62) is a high school principal planning to retire in two years. Together they bring complementary income sources and a strong savings history built over 35 years.

Their accounts are custodied at Schwab. Robert has a rollover IRA ($1.4M), a taxable brokerage account ($380K), and a small 401(k) from consulting work ($62K). Susan has a 403(b) from the school district ($520K) and a pension that will pay approximately $3,200/month at her projected retirement age of 64. Their financial plan lives in RightCapital; tax projections run through Holistiplan; risk profiling through DataPoints.

They own their home outright (estimated value $740K) and carry no debt.

---

## Goals

1. **Retire at 66 (Robert) and 64 (Susan).** Robert is already retired from his primary career but still does occasional consulting. The target is full income replacement from portfolio and Social Security by 2026.
2. **Fund grandchildren's 529 plans.** Three grandchildren, ages 2, 4, and 7. They want to seed each account with $30,000 over the next three years and revisit each year.
3. **Leave a legacy.** They have earmarked $250,000 for a charitable remainder trust to fund a scholarship at Susan's alma mater. No timeline pressure, but they want the structure in place before Susan retires.
4. **Maintain current lifestyle.** Monthly spending is approximately $9,200. They expect this to hold steady in early retirement and drop slightly after age 75.
