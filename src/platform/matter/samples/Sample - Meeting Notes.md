## Decisions Made

**Roth conversion:** We agreed to convert a portion of Robert's rollover IRA to a Roth IRA this year. The target conversion amount is approximately $48,000, which takes them to the top of the 24% bracket without triggering the 32% rate. Robert will initiate the conversion at Schwab in Q4 to give us time to finalize the Holistiplan projections. We will revisit the conversion strategy each year through Susan's retirement.

**529 funding:** Deferred to next year. With Susan's exact retirement date and pension income still being finalized, we agreed it makes more sense to model the 529 contributions against confirmed post-retirement cash flow. Robert is keeping the conversation open with his son about the grandchildren's school timeline.

**Beneficiary update:** Robert's IRA and taxable brokerage account still list his mother as primary beneficiary. She passed in September 2022. Robert agreed to update both accounts at Schwab before their daughter's wedding in June. Susan is the intended primary for all accounts; the three grandchildren equally as contingent.

---

## Other Topics Covered

- **Susan's pension:** The estimate letter confirmed $3,210/month at age 64 (full benefit) or $2,890/month if she takes it at 62. We modeled both scenarios in RightCapital. The $320/month difference compounds significantly over a long retirement; we recommended she wait for the full benefit unless health changes the calculus.
- **Social Security timing:** Robert is eligible now at 64. We reviewed the breakeven analysis. At his life expectancy assumption (87), delaying to 70 adds approximately $215,000 in lifetime benefits compared to claiming at 64. He wants to delay. Susan plans to take her benefit at 67 (her full retirement age).
- **Long-term care insurance:** Susan's group coverage through the school district ends when she retires. We flagged this as a 2025 priority. Premiums are lower before age 65. We have a call scheduled with an LTC specialist for May.
- **Portfolio drift:** The current 65/35 allocation has drifted to 68/32 due to equity growth in 2023. We will rebalance in the next 60 days back to the target. No changes to the underlying holdings at this time.
