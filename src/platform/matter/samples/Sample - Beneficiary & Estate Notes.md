## Charitable Remainder Trust (Planned)

The Hendricks have earmarked $250,000 for a charitable remainder trust (CRT) to fund a scholarship at Susan's alma mater. Key planning notes:

- No hard deadline, but the goal is to have the structure in place before Susan retires at 64 (approximately 2026)
- A CRT provides income to the Hendricks during their lifetimes and transfers the remainder to the named charity
- The estate attorney referral (pending) will cover CRT structure, the charitable deduction, and coordination with required minimum distributions from Robert's IRA
- $250,000 is earmarked; exact funding source (IRA assets vs. taxable brokerage) to be determined after the estate attorney review

## Required Minimum Distribution Notes

Robert will reach RMD age (73) in 2033. The Roth conversion strategy is designed in part to reduce the size of his pre-tax IRA before RMDs begin, which lowers future ordinary income and the associated Medicare surcharge exposure. The charitable remainder trust, if funded from IRA assets, may provide an additional tool for managing the RMD burden at that time.

Susan's 403(b) RMDs will also begin at age 73 (2035), unless she is still employed by the school district, in which case RMDs on that account may be deferred.
