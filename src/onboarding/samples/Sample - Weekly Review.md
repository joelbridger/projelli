- Pro users want a "pause subscription" option for slow months. Three people asked this week. I'd been saying no because it hurts MRR predictability, but the right answer might be a one-click 30-day pause that auto-resumes. Putting it on the board.
- Freelancers don't read onboarding emails. They watch onboarding videos on 1.5x speed. Every copy change I made this month moved the needle less than the video re-record did.
- The word "tax" in the hero headline outperforms "money" by 40% in conversion. Swapped the headline Thursday.
