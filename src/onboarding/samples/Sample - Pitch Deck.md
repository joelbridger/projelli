# Sample: Pitch Deck

> Fictional product: **Acme Budget**, a personal finance app for freelancers.
> This is a sample deck outline so you can see what a finished PitchDeck run looks like. Delete it whenever you want.

## Slide 1: Title

- Acme Budget
- Personal finance for the 76 million Americans who work for themselves
- Seed round, $1.5M

## Slide 2: The problem

- Freelancers get audited at 2x the rate of W-2 employees
- 43% of 1099 filers owe more than they expected at tax time
- Existing tools (Mint, YNAB, spreadsheets) were built for salaries, not irregular income
- The pain is quiet until April, then it's a crisis

## Slide 3: Who we serve

- Solo designers, writers, developers, consultants, freelance lawyers, and fractional operators
- They already pay for tools (Figma, Notion, Harvest) but no one's serving their money stack
- Our ICP: 1-3 years freelance, $40K-$150K gross income, tax-anxious

## Slide 4: Our insight

- Freelancers don't need more tracking. They need fewer April surprises.
- Every other finance app treats tax as a report at year-end
- We treat tax as a live dashboard all year

## Slide 5: The product

- Live quarterly tax estimator that updates with every transaction
- Auto-categorization trained on 1099 expense patterns, not W-2 patterns
- CPA-share mode: one link, view-only, no tax-software import needed
- Mobile-first receipt capture (you're on a job site, not at a desk)

## Slide 6: Traction

- 1,200 signups in 4 months with $0 paid acquisition
- 180 paying customers at $9/month, $1,620 MRR
- 97% weekly active rate among paying customers (they actually use it)
- NPS 52 at 90 days

## Slide 7: Market

- 76M US self-employed workers, IRS 2024 data
- Growing 4% a year, outpacing W-2 employment
- SAM: 8M freelancers with $40K+ gross income
- SOM at 1% capture: 80K customers, $8.6M ARR at $9/mo

## Slide 8: Competition

- QuickBooks Self-Employed: $20/mo, clunky, built for accountants, hated
- YNAB: $14.99/mo, great product, zero tax features
- FreshBooks: $17/mo, invoicing-first, finance is a bolt-on
- Mint: free, shutting down, no replacement loyalty

We sit in the gap: tax-native, freelancer-native, affordable.

## Slide 9: Business model

- Free tier (acquisition), Solo $9/mo, Pro $19/mo
- Gross margin 82% at scale (Plaid fees + hosting)
- LTV/CAC trending 4.2x on organic
- Annual plans 61% of revenue already

## Slide 10: The ask

- $1.5M on a $10M SAFE cap
- 18-month runway
- Hire: 1 mobile engineer, 1 growth marketer
- Milestones: 10K paying customers by end of Year 2, break even at 15K

## Slide 11: Why us

- I spent 6 years at Intuit building QuickBooks Self-Employed and watched leadership deprioritize it every quarter
- My cofounder ran a freelance agency for 8 years and has been the target customer
- We know this market cold

## Slide 12: Closing

- Freelancing isn't going away. Their tools haven't caught up.
- We're building the finance OS for the self-employed decade.
- Thank you.
