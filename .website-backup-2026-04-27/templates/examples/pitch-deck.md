# Acme Budget, Pitch Deck Outline

## Slide 1: Title

**Acme Budget**
A personal finance app that thinks like a freelancer.
Maya Chen, Founder. maya@acmebudget.com.

## Slide 2: The problem

73% of US freelancers miss at least one quarterly tax payment every two years, according to [the IRS's own estimated-tax data](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/estimated-taxes). The existing solutions were built for W-2 earners with predictable paychecks.

One image: a chart showing a freelancer's actual monthly income across 12 months, spiky and irregular, overlaid with the flat line YNAB and Copilot assume.

## Slide 3: The market

- 73.3 million independent workers in the US ([MBO Partners 2025 State of Independence](https://www.mbopartners.com/state-of-independence/))
- 14.4 million "full-time independents" earning >$60K/year (the sharp ICP)
- Growing at 5.2% CAGR since 2020
- TAM for a $12/mo product against this narrow slice: $2.1B/year

## Slide 4: The insight

Every freelancer I interviewed does the same thing manually: when an invoice clears, they move ~30% to a savings account, they tell themselves this is tax money, and within 3 months they've spent it. The problem is not knowledge. It's execution. Automating the allocation the moment the money arrives is the whole product.

## Slide 7: Competitive landscape

Quadrant chart: x-axis "built for freelancers vs built for W-2", y-axis "does auto tax allocation". Acme Budget is the only tool in the upper-right quadrant. YNAB and Copilot sit in lower-left. Keeper and Found sit upper-left (tax, but not full budgeting).

## Slide 8: Business model

$12/month, $108/year. Target 78% gross margin after Plaid, Stripe, hosting. $85/year contribution margin per customer. 30-day refund, no questions. No freemium, no enterprise.

## Slide 9: Go-to-market

SEO on freelance tax queries (12,100 searches/month for the top 3 queries), accountant partnerships (30 CPAs targeted), and niche-community Twitter/LinkedIn. No paid ads in year one.
