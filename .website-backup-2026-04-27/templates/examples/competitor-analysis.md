# Acme Budget, Competitor Analysis

## The landscape

The personal finance app space has three shapes. Each one leaves room for a freelancer-specific product.

### Shape 1: Budgeting apps for W-2 earners

[YNAB](https://www.ynab.com/), [Monarch](https://www.monarchmoney.com/), and [Copilot](https://copilot.money/) all assume predictable monthly income. Zero-based budgeting works when you get paid on the 15th and the 30th. It doesn't work when a client's invoice is 45 days late and another just paid early.

### Shape 2: Bookkeeping for small businesses

[QuickBooks Self-Employed](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/self-employed/), [Wave](https://www.waveapps.com/), and [Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com/) handle invoicing, receipts, and Schedule C prep. They're accounting tools. They don't do personal budgeting, they don't forecast runway, and they cost $15-35/month.

### Shape 3: Tax-set-aside tools

[Keeper](https://www.keepertax.com/) and [Found](https://found.com/) are the closest to what I'm building. Keeper focuses on write-off discovery. Found is a bundled banking + tax-set-aside product. Neither does full personal budgeting.

## Key gaps Acme Budget is filling

1. **No tool combines freelancer-aware budgeting with auto tax-set-aside.** Keeper is closest but stops at tax. YNAB is closest on budgeting but fights variable income.
2. **Nobody shows runway in weeks.** This is the number freelancers actually care about. "If I invoice nothing new starting today, how long can I cover my expenses?"
3. **Pricing is crowded at $13-15/month.** Acme Budget lives inside that band but ships a clearer, narrower feature set.

## Where each competitor wins

- **YNAB**: best for hardcore budgeters with stable income. The method is a real thing with a real community.
- **Copilot**: cleanest design in the category, but Mac-only and not freelancer-aware.
- **Found**: if you want banking + tax bundled and you're comfortable switching your primary account, this is it.
- **QuickBooks SE**: the only one your accountant already understands.

## Positioning move

Acme Budget is not for everyone who freelances. It's for the 1-3 year freelancer who wants to keep their existing bank and handle their own books, but has been burned by a surprise tax bill and wants their income split automatically the moment it hits their account.

The single sentence: *Acme Budget is the budgeting app that assumes your income is lumpy, your tax bill is terrifying, and you need to know your runway in weeks, not your net worth in dollars.*
