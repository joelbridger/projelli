## How it works

Three steps, one screenshot each:

1. **Connect your bank.** We use [Plaid](https://plaid.com/) (same as Venmo, Robinhood, SoFi). Read-only. We never move money.
2. **Set your three percentages.** 28% for taxes, 10% for emergency, 62% spendable is our default. Adjust once and we recalculate quarterly based on your actual effective tax rate.
3. **Get paid, get calm.** Every income event is auto-allocated the moment it clears. You see three separate balances. The tax money lives in a tracked bucket you can see but know not to touch.

## FAQ

1. **Is my data safe?** Acme Budget reads your transactions via Plaid read-only access. We never have your bank password, and we cannot move money. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
2. **What if Plaid doesn't support my bank?** We have a manual CSV import flow. Takes 30 seconds per month.
3. **Is this a bank account?** No. Acme Budget is a software layer on top of your existing bank. You keep your checking, you keep your savings, we just read transactions and compute the buckets.
4. **What happens to my tax reserve when it's time to pay the IRS?** We show you the exact amount to transfer from your designated tax-savings account to the IRS. We don't move the money for you. That's deliberate.
5. **Do you support state taxes?** Yes, for the 7 states with the highest freelancer populations in v1.0. All 50 by Q3.
6. **What if I want to cancel?** Cancel anywhere in the app, effective immediately. We keep your data for 30 days in case you come back.
