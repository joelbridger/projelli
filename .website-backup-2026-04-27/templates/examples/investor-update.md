## The user interviews

Ran 10 interviews total across March and April. Three patterns emerged cleanly:

1. **Every single freelancer has a "tax account" that gets raided.** This validated the core product thesis.
2. **Nobody can name their effective hourly rate across all their clients.** This is my first sighting of the next feature I'll build (profit-by-client view).
3. **Everyone checks their bank balance 4-6 times a day.** The Sunday email replaces this for ~60% of beta users after 30 days.
