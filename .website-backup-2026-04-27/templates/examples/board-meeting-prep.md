### Decision 1: credit card for free trial

**Context.** Today, the 14-day trial requires no card. The hypothesis is that freelancers are card-averse and we'd lose trials if we asked.

**New data.** Ran an A/B test in April-May. 180 trial signups in the no-card arm, 135 in the card-required arm. Conversion: 24% no-card, 44% card. Net paid per 100 trial visits: 24 vs 33.

**The decision.** Switch to card-required. Net paid-users-per-dollar-of-SEO-effort is 38% higher.

**What I need from the board.** Agreement or objection. The decision is mostly made but I want the pushback before I rip out the funnel.

### Decision 2: iOS engineer

**Context.** Three paid users have asked for a mobile app. The desktop web app works on iOS Safari but it's not a native experience.

**The hire candidate.** Contract-to-hire, 3 months at $14,000/month, one ex-Monarch iOS lead who's looking for side work. Total spend: $42K for a beta iOS app by Q4.

**The risk.** Spending 3 months of runway on a feature that the public validation is 3 users. If Acme Budget is at $5K MRR in September and I've burned $42K on iOS, I've shortened my personal runway by 4-5 months.

**My lean.** Wait until $10K MRR. At that point I can pay the $42K out of revenue rather than savings.

**What I need from the board.** Argue me out of waiting if I should be more aggressive, argue me into waiting if I'm already there.
