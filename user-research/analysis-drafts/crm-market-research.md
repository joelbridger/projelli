## 1. Wealthbox today

**Pricing** (accessed 2026-07): Basic $59/user/mo, Pro $75/user/mo, Premier $99/user/mo
(monthly billing); with annual billing Basic $35, Pro $49, Premier $65/user/mo. Enterprise
custom. Sources: wealthbox.com/pricing, Capterra.

**Scale/funding:** $200M strategic majority investment from Sixth Street Growth, announced
2025-06-10 — total raised $231M. Stated use: accelerate AI features, deepen enterprise
functionality, broaden integrations (InvestmentNews 2025-06; fintech.global 2025-06-11).
Headcount ~145 as of 2026-03-31 (Tracxn/PitchBook — UNVERIFIED precision). Example
enterprise win: Gradient Financial Group, 2,000+ users, CRM + AI Notetaker (PRNewswire,
2026-01). No independently audited total-customer count found.

**AI product moves:** first AI notetaker ("Project Althea") shipped fall 2025 (announced
2025-05-14, InvestmentNews). March 2026 (Future Proof): early access to **Agents**
(background automations), **Playbooks** (saved multi-step workflow prompts), and an **AI
Assistant** (conversational client/pipeline Q&A) — pitched as moving the CRM "beyond a
system of record into a system of action" (PRNewswire/Wealthbox 2026-03). April 2026:
Notetaker native in-person iOS recording, automatic template selection (wealthbox.com,
2026-04).

**Ecosystem / Jump:** deep two-way Jump integration — Jump builds pre-meeting briefs from
Wealthbox records and pushes notes/tasks/workflow triggers back; users report meeting admin
dropping from 30-40 min to 3-4 min (wealthbox.com; jump.ai/integrations/wealthbox). Amplify
added Wealthbox to its multi-custodial platform 2026-06 (fintech.global).

## 2. Advisor CRM market share

- **2025 T3/Inside Information survey:** 86% of advisors use a CRM; **Redtail #1 at
  26.32%, Wealthbox #2 at 21.62%**. Wealthbox reportedly dominates the solo/small-firm
  segment (under $250K revenue); Redtail stronger in $250K-$1M multi-advisor firms (Kitces
  AdvisorTech roundups 2025 — segment claim from aggregated coverage, light caution).
- **2026 T3 survey** (2,906 respondents, published ~2026-03-10): overall CRM adoption
  91.08% (down from >96% in 2023); 17 CRM products measured; **Advyzon 10.19%,
  third-highest**; CRM rated the most valuable software category. Vendor-by-vendor 2026
  numbers for Wealthbox/Redtail not broken out in accessible coverage — **follow up with
  the raw PDF if precise 2026 shares matter**. "Orion leads the category" claims conflate
  Orion (parent) with Redtail (product) — UNVERIFIED.
- **Trend:** 2024 coverage (FamilyWealthReport 2024-01-29) had Redtail 45% / Wealthbox 14%;
  against 2025's 26.3%/21.6% this indicates multi-year Redtail erosion and Wealthbox
  growth. Directional (single secondary source).
- **Satisfaction:** Wealthbox cites a 93% "excellent" aggregate across ~252 reviews
  (SelectHub — vendor-favorable). **Advyzon ranked #1 all-in-one satisfaction for the 9th
  consecutive year** (2026 T3; t3technologyhub.com).

## 3. CRM switching

- Advisor-specific CRMs (Redtail, Wealthbox) commonly deploy in **1-2 weeks** basic;
  Salesforce FSC implementations 3-6 months; general mid-size migrations 10-20 weeks
  (Vantage Point blog 2026 — vendor-adjacent, directional).
- Costs: $45-300+/user/mo across platforms; implementations $5K-$200K+; simple migrations
  from ~$25K; migration-support quotes $500-$2,000 (+$200-800 for export consulting on
  complex Salesforce instances). Most common failure: underestimating data-migration effort
  (recommend 2-4× time budget) (gainaltitude.ai; Vantage Point 2026 — vendor content,
  treat as industry-typical).
- Anecdote: a G2 reviewer called a Redtail→Wealthbox migration "quick and mostly seamless."
  **No indexed r/CFP, r/RIA, or XYPN-forum switching horror stories found** — a gap in
  what's searchable, not proof of absence. UNVERIFIED / not found.

## 4. Consolidation appetite vs all-in-one skepticism

- Schwab's 2025 Independent Advisor Outlook Study (cited secondhand in 2026 coverage —
  primary not reached): **37% of RIAs run a "hybrid" stack** (integrated core + best-of-
  breed tools) — the most common configuration; 94% of practice-management professionals
  say limited tool integration creates productivity problems; 57% of advisors call poor
  integration their most significant tech pain point (oncehub.com 2026 — secondary,
  directional).
- Skepticism datapoint: Advyzon announced financial-planning plans for 2026; Kitces'
  2025-11 roundup notes practitioner skepticism given the "spotty history" of fusing
  portfolio management + CRM + planning into one product.

## 6. Whitespace check

- **No local-first, self-hosted, or E2EE CRM purpose-built for financial advisors found.**
  2026 RIA software roundups list only cloud SaaS (SmartRIA, Comply, Orion Compliance,
  Greenboard, Advyzon, Wealthbox, Redtail, Salesforce FSC).
- Generic self-hosted CRMs exist (e.g. Grow CRM, $39 lifetime, privacy-marketed) — not
  finance-specific, no SEC recordkeeping claims (growcrm.io, accessed 2026-07).
- Adjacent: healthcare's "HIPAA-compliant CRM" category (Kustomer, Caspio, Symplast,
  PostGrid — 2026-current) markets encryption, access controls, audit trails, BAAs — but
  all cloud-hosted; differentiation is compliance posture, not data locality. Legal-vertical
  privacy CRM: not found in this pass — UNVERIFIED.
- Conclusion: whitespace appears real; "didn't find one" ≠ proof. A dedicated exhaustive
  pass (Kitces AdvisorTech Map, T3 directory, G2/Capterra category crawl) is needed before
  treating it as confirmed for a strategic bet.

## 7. Recordkeeping note (NOT legal advice)

- RIAs: **Advisers Act Rule 204-2** — records kept 5 years, first 2 readily accessible
  (Cornell LII / Smarsh). SEC Rule 17a-4 (WORM etc.) applies to broker-dealers, not RIAs —
  vendor marketing often blurs this.
- 17a-4 was modernized to allow an **audit-trail alternative** to strict WORM (every
  change/access logged; originals verifiably reconstructable) — coverage via Davis Polk /
  Archive360; exact dates not pinned in this pass, flag for a date check.
- Practitioner framing: the FIRM stays accountable for records regardless of vendor;
  advisors are told to vet CRM/archiving for WORM-equivalent storage and audit trails
  (smart-ria.com, globalrelay.com, accessed 2026-07). **A compliance attorney must confirm
  what a system of record needs under Rule 204-2 before any product decision.**
