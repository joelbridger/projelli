# 00 — Executive synthesis: advisor product-value session, 2026-07-10

*~116 minutes with a practicing advisor (client-service/ops role, ~6-person RIA, XYPN
network; uses Jump, Wealthbox, RightCapital, Schwab, Calendly, Outlook, Teams, OneDrive).
She is the founder's wife, a prior collaborator, with prior product exposure — this session
is deep workflow insight and hypothesis generation, NOT market validation. The July 8th
session tested pillar 1 ("can we use it?" — compliance); this one bracketed compliance as
approved and tested pillar 2 ("would it be helpful?"). Full traceability: every claim below
cites the evidence ledger (01, E-###); methods and limits in 02 and 07.*

---

## The eight most consequential insights

**1. The center of gravity is "what's the latest and greatest?" — cross-source,
dated, cited.** Her own scenario: the umbrella policy is $1M in a OneDrive file but $2M in a
later email; she wants one answer that reconciles both *with the dates* and the proof
(E-066/E-067). It recurred as the HSA/benefits question (E-069), the planner's "what is the
source of truth... scavenger hunt" complaint (E-104), and her closing "hugely valuable for
our firm" (E-128). `source-of-truth` was the most frequent code in the session (25 of 129
evidence items). This is Ask's job, and the missing piece is specific: dates carried through
retrieval, recency-aware answers, conflict flags. (High confidence — her own examples,
minimal leading.)

**2. The deepest pain is emotional: "we all are living in this horrible fear that we are
going to be the one that drops the ball" (E-053).** Information moves through fragile human
relays — advisor → template notes → her → Wealthbox tasks → planner → RightCapital ("it's a
lot of hands", E-049); color lives in Teams and memory (E-021, E-051); real harms have
happened (wrong Roth IRA changed, E-076; 1099-to-wrong-accountant is "a nail biter", E-094).
The product's real job is dread reduction: fewer manual hops, disambiguation, durable capture.

**3. Verification is a law, and approval must interrupt — she will not read logs.**
Nothing derived from a meeting moves until a human who was present green-lights it (E-027,
E-036). Offered approval-gates vs audit-logs, she chose approval with a reason that should
shape the whole UX: "I've got a lot to do... that's maybe just one more thing to think about
doing" (E-062). Review must cost seconds (include/exclude checkboxes), because advisor
review bandwidth is the scarcest resource (E-038; Chris abandons Jump's notes entirely when
the format breaks and rewrites from memory, E-039).

**4. The wedge against Jump is template fidelity + safe routing — the exact thing Jump told
them it can't do.** The firm is "married to" its 3-part note template, refused to flex it
even to unlock automation, and Jump confirmed in a recent meeting it still can't turn their
template into tasks (E-030, E-032). Meanwhile the template systematically loses the "human
story" — relational color, the last-10-minutes reversals (E-041), the kid's college (E-024)
— which they want in a SECOND, internal-only, searchable artifact that must never reach the
client (E-050–E-052; transcript-to-client is a liability fear, E-034). Faithful firm-template
notes + per-item review routed to tasks/internal lane is a build we're unusually close to
(06 #10–12).

**5. Privacy didn't come up. At all.** With compliance bracketed as approved, she never once
asked for local, private, or "nothing leaves my machine" — the entire 07-08 headline was
absent from her pillar-2 wants. Read together, the two sessions say: **privacy/local-first is
the permission to buy; capability (cited, current answers; safe routing) is the reason to
want.** The pitch should open the gate with compliance and sell the house with capability —
not the other way around. (Skeptic lane's strongest finding; see 02 T7, 05 D5.)

**6. The Client Map, as a maintained artifact, took real damage this session.** Every
"map" framing was interviewer-introduced; she adopted the word only after it was offered,
hedged ("I don't know if that's worth building", E-071), and named the kill condition
herself: "if you were to go visit it and it was out of date, it'd probably decrease trust a
lot" (E-072) — against her meta-pain, "we have so many places to keep updated" (E-104), a
tended map is one more place. The underlying machinery (completeness, gaps, sourced facts)
remains valuable as INPUT; the headline should shift from "a living map you visit" to
"answers computed from live sources at the moment you ask, always dated." (This challenges
current flagship language — decision for Jameson, 08 D2.)

**7. Wealthbox is the firm's operating system — coexist and write back; don't chase
replacement from this evidence.** It's "integral" (E-109), it's also structurally dumb in
ways that matter daily (keyword-brittle search E-019, unsearchable email E-020, dumb task
lists E-119, template edits that don't propagate to 40 open workflows E-098/E-099). Her
"I've been hoping you'd just replace Wealthbox and Jump" (E-110) is real pull but
spouse-invested and contradicted by her own dependence testimony (E-106–E-109) — park
replacement; win by being the intelligence on top with approval-gated writes into their real
containers (assignees, workflows — neither supported today, 06 #14).

**8. Proactivity is welcome exactly where it can't go stale: triage, nudges, reports-on-
demand.** The morning task-triage "coach" (dumb overdue reds; a spelling task looks like a
fraud situation, E-119; capacity honesty "would be helpful" even if shaming, E-121), the
scheduling cascade (Calendly's rules force ~40% manual Outlook booking; whose-link/service-
tier knowledge lives in her head, E-080–E-086), and the engagement-gap report (E-088) all
drew genuine pull. The rule that reconciles this with #6: **compute on view, date visibly,
propose — never present a stored artifact as current.**

## What changed in our understanding
- The value story's spine moved from "a living Client Map" to **"dated, cited,
  cross-source answers + safe routing into the firm's own systems."**
- "Meeting prep" as we imagined it (auto-briefs) was never requested; prep at this firm is
  slide-deck updating, Schwab checks, and recall (E-070, E-101) — briefs may still be right,
  but this session doesn't support them as the hook.
- Intake went from "new feature" to **strategic credibility engine** ("never ask twice" is a
  client-experience economy, T7) — and it's already built, awaiting merge (06 #22).
- The competitive wedge against Jump sharpened from "more private notetaker" to **"the one
  that fits your firm exactly"** (template fidelity, two audiences, your CRM containers).

## What was reinforced
- Approval-first / AI-proposes-user-approves (the product constitution) — emphatically
  (T3).
- Local capture without a meeting bot (their inbound-call and light-seat frictions,
  E-055/E-056).
- Connector choices: email + Wealthbox + OneDrive + Calendly are exactly her firm's
  bloodstream (every workflow stage touched them).
- The board's simplicity stance — complexity and volume are named battery drains
  (E-038/E-039).

## What was challenged
- **"Living Client Map" as flagship language** (insight 6; decision 08 D2).
- **Word-native/redline emphasis for advisors** — her firm barely uses Word (E-060);
  PowerPoint decks are their client-facing artifact (E-100–E-102; slide OCR is the unlock,
  06 #28).
- **The engagement-report scope-out** — a real user asked for exactly what the needs-
  discovery doc excluded as "CRM-shaped" (E-088 vs 06 #6); needs a deliberate boundary
  decision (08 D6).
- **"Every answer is cited" as sufficient** — without dates, citations answer "where from?"
  but not her actual question, "is this current?" (E-067, E-072).

## The biggest product opportunity
**The Source of Truth release** (05): dates-through-retrieval timeline answers (★B1) +
firm-template meeting outputs with routed, approval-gated task extraction (★B2 + B3) +
the intake merge (B4). One coherent story — *ask anything, get the current answer with
proof; end a meeting, get your notes in your format and your tasks in your CRM, approved by
you* — demoable with the two scenarios she designed herself (umbrella; HSA).

## The biggest product risk
**Trust collapse through staleness or leakage.** She named both kill conditions: a stale
answer/artifact ("I can't trust this system", E-072) and internal color reaching a client
(E-034/E-050). Both are one-strike failures for the product's core promise. Every surface
this research motivates must show its dates and hard-separate internal from client-facing
content. Secondary risk: building her co-authored solution shapes (coach, maps, manager
reports) as if they were validated demand — they are hypotheses from one atypical
participant (07).

## Recommended immediate decisions (detail in 08)
1. Adopt the Source of Truth release theme (05 sequencing) as the near-term focus.
2. Decide `lp/intake` merge timing (finished, verified work delivering the session's
   most-loved concept is sitting on a side branch).
3. Reframe flagship language from maintained-map to computed-dated-answers (marketing +
   demo assets).
4. Approve the follow-up research plan (07) and take her recruiting offer (XYPN group,
   honoraria) — with a non-founder moderator for at least half the sessions.
5. Apply the B5 copy fixes (repeat confusions from 07-08 included).

## What should NOT be decided from this interview alone
- Anything priced, and anything about what a BUYER (firm owner/advisor) values — she is ops,
  not the buyer (E-116/E-117 second-hand personas; 07 D3).
- Replace-Wealthbox ambitions in any direction (F5).
- Whether the coach, manager reports, portal, or cash-needs records deserve builds
  (co-authored/hedged evidence; C1/E1–E3 gates).
- Whether template lock-in and the scheduling cascade generalize beyond this firm (D2, 07 §6).
- Privacy's final place in the pitch order — the 07-08 vs 07-10 tension needs stranger
  testing (D5).
