Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement — Nexus Diagnostics Inc. and Helix Bioworks LLC

This mutual non-disclosure agreement is between Nexus Diagnostics Inc., a clinical laboratory, and Helix Bioworks LLC, and governs a joint research evaluation of a diagnostic assay.

The agreement includes a special provision the parties call the Telomere Assay Confidentiality Rider, which protects the proprietary methodology of the TA-204 telomere-length assay.

All confidential information must be returned or destroyed within thirty days of termination, and the Telomere Assay Confidentiality Rider survives for seven years after the agreement ends.

The agreement provides for liquidated damages of $25,000 for each unauthorized disclosure of the TA-204 assay methodology.
