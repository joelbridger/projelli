Engagement Letter — Marchetti & Associates LLP

This engagement letter is between Marchetti & Associates LLP and the client, Marcus Johnson. The responsible attorney is Diane Marchetti.

The scope of the engagement is limited to a wrongful-termination claim against Nexus Dynamics Corp. The firm is not engaged for any tax, criminal, or family-law matter.

The client agrees to an hourly billing rate of $425 per hour for attorney time and $150 per hour for paralegal time.

The client agrees to pay an initial retainer of $10,000, to be held in the firm's trust account and applied against fees as they are incurred.

Either party may terminate the engagement with fourteen days' written notice.
