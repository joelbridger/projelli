Incident Summary — Johnson v. Nexus Dynamics Corp.

Prepared by the Nexus Dynamics legal team. This internal summary describes the company's account of the events leading to Marcus Johnson's termination.

The summary states that no documents ever left company systems and that all materials remained on company servers only throughout the relevant period.

The summary states that the compliance team gave Johnson a written-response deadline of October 10, 2025.

The summary states that Johnson was offered a severance package of eight weeks of pay, consistent with company policy for employees with five or more years of service.

The summary states that Johnson received two verbal performance warnings in August 2025.
