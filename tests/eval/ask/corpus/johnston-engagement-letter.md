Engagement Letter — Brunswick Legal Group

This engagement letter is between Brunswick Legal Group and the client, Marcus Johnston. The responsible attorney is Theodore Brunswick.

The scope of the engagement is limited to a wrongful-termination claim against Pinnacle Logistics Inc. The firm is not engaged for any tax, criminal, or immigration matter.

The client agrees to an hourly billing rate of $390 per hour for attorney time and $135 per hour for paralegal time.

The client agrees to pay an initial retainer of $7,500, to be held in the firm's trust account and applied against fees as they are incurred.

Either party may terminate the engagement with thirty days' written notice.
