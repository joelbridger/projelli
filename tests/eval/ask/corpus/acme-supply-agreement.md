Supply Agreement — Acme Corporation and Road Runner Logistics LLC

This supply agreement is between Acme Corporation (the buyer) and Road Runner Logistics LLC (the carrier). It governs the delivery of Acme's flagship product.

The agreement has a term of twenty-four months from the effective date, and renews automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives ninety days' notice.

The product covered by this agreement is the Widget Model X, to be shipped from Acme's Albuquerque warehouse to distribution centers nationwide.

The agreement provides for liquidated damages of $500 per day for each shipment that Road Runner delivers later than the agreed delivery window.

Road Runner warrants that it carries cargo insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence for the duration of the agreement.
