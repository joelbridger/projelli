Deposition of Marcus Johnston — Johnston v. Pinnacle Logistics Inc.

Taken on December 2, 2025. Marcus Johnston, the plaintiff, is the former warehouse supervisor suing Pinnacle Logistics Inc. for wrongful termination. Theodore Brunswick appeared for the plaintiff.

Johnston testified that on August 21, 2025 he downloaded several shift-scheduling records to a personal USB drive before his badge was deactivated.

Johnston testified that the human resources team gave him a written-response deadline of November 3, 2025 to answer the safety-violation allegations.

Johnston testified that Pinnacle Logistics offered him a severance package equal to six weeks of pay, which he accepted.

Johnston testified that his direct supervisor, Priya Anand, documented two written warnings before the termination meeting.
