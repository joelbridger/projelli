Deposition of Marcus Johnson — Johnson v. Nexus Dynamics Corp.

Taken on November 4, 2025. Marcus Johnson, the plaintiff, is the former operations analyst suing Nexus Dynamics Corp. for wrongful termination. Diane Marchetti appeared for the plaintiff.

Johnson testified that on September 9 and 10, 2025 he forwarded several internal compliance documents to his personal email account for safekeeping before his access was revoked.

Johnson testified that the compliance team gave him a written-response deadline of October 17, 2025 to answer their questions about the audit.

Johnson testified that Nexus Dynamics offered him a severance package equal to four weeks of pay, which he declined.

Johnson testified that his direct supervisor, Karen Vance, never put any performance complaints in writing before the termination meeting.
