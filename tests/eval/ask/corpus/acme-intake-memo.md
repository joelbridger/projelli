Client Intake Memo — Acme Corporation v. Road Runner Logistics LLC

The client is Acme Corporation, represented by its chief executive officer, Wile E. Coyote. The matter is a contract dispute arising from supply-chain disruption.

The claim is that Road Runner Logistics LLC breached the supply agreement by repeatedly delivering Widget Model X shipments outside the agreed delivery window during the first and second quarters of 2025.

Acme seeks liquidated damages under the agreement plus consequential damages for lost retail sales during the affected quarters.

The intake notes that Road Runner has signaled it will argue a force-majeure defense based on a regional fuel shortage.
